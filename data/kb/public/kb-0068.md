The Quillstack mobile app gives you access to your workspaces, pages, projects, and tasks from iOS and Android devices. You can create and edit content even when you're offline, and your changes sync automatically when you reconnect. This article covers the system requirements, how offline mode works, and what you can and cannot do from the mobile app.

## Before you start

The mobile app requires iOS 16 or later for iPhones and iPads, or Android 11 or later for Android phones and tablets. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play Store by searching for "Quillstack". Sign in with the same credentials you use at app.quillstack.com. If your workspace uses SSO, the app redirects you to your identity provider for authentication.

Your mobile device needs at least 500 MB of free storage to install the app and cache workspace data for offline use. The app works on phones and tablets, but some features like split-screen editing and multi-column project views are optimized for tablets and larger screens.

## Steps

### Sign in and access your workspaces

1. Open the Quillstack app and tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and password, or tap **Sign in with SSO** if your workspace uses single sign-on.
3. Complete any two-factor authentication prompts if 2FA is enabled on your account.
4. Select the workspace you want to open from the workspace list.

Once signed in, you stay signed in for up to 90 days of inactivity. If you belong to multiple workspaces, tap the workspace name at the top of the screen to switch between them.

### Work offline and sync changes

1. Open any page or project while you have an internet connection; the app caches it for offline access.
2. When you go offline, continue editing pages and tasks as usual. A banner at the top of the screen shows "Offline mode".
3. Reconnect to the internet. The app syncs your changes automatically within a few seconds.

Offline edits sync on reconnect with conflicts resolved as last write wins. If you and a teammate both edit the same page offline, the last person to reconnect overwrites earlier changes. To avoid losing work, check the page version history after syncing if you suspect a conflict occurred.

The app caches up to 200 recently viewed pages and the 50 most recent tasks across all projects. If you need a page or task that is not cached, you must be online to load it. Attachments larger than 10 MB are not cached for offline use and require a connection to view.

## How offline mode handles different content types

The table below shows what you can do offline and what requires a connection:

| Action | Offline | Requires connection |
|--------|---------|---------------------|
| View cached pages and tasks | Yes | No |
| Edit page content | Yes | No |
| Create new pages | Yes | No |
| Update task status | Yes | No |
| Upload attachments | No | Yes |
| Download attachments over 10 MB | No | Yes |
| Search across the workspace | No | Yes |
| Invite members | No | Yes |
| View audit log | No | Yes |

When you create a new page or task offline, it receives a temporary ID until the app syncs and the server assigns a permanent ID. You cannot share a page or copy its public link until it syncs and gets a permanent URL.

## Limitations

Billing settings cannot be changed from the mobile app, only at app.quillstack.com. To upgrade your plan, add seats, update your payment method, or view invoices, sign in to the web app from a browser on any device. The mobile app shows your current plan and seat count under Settings > Billing, but all changes must be made on the web.

The mobile app does not support workspace imports, exports, or bulk operations. To import content from Notion, Confluence, or Markdown files, or to export your workspace as a .zip archive, use the web app at app.quillstack.com.

API keys cannot be created, rotated, or revoked from the mobile app. Manage API keys under Settings > Integrations > API keys in the web app. Webhook configuration and integration settings for Slack and GitHub are also web-only features.

## Troubleshooting

If your changes do not sync after reconnecting, force a sync by pulling down on the page or task list to refresh. If that does not work, sign out and sign back in. Your offline edits are stored locally and will sync once the connection is restored.

If the app crashes or freezes when opening a large page, the page may contain too many embedded attachments or database rows. Open the page in the web app and split it into smaller pages, then try again on mobile.

For persistent problems, see the detailed troubleshooting steps in the mobile app troubleshooting article.
