Quillstack offers three plans to fit teams of every size, from small groups trying out the product to large organizations with advanced security requirements. Each plan includes pages, projects, and task tracking, with higher tiers adding storage, integrations, and features like SSO. This article compares the plans so you can choose the right one for your team.

## Before you start

All new accounts start on the Free plan. You can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing, and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. If you are not sure which plan you need, start with Free and upgrade when you hit a limit or need a feature that is not available on your current plan.

## Plans compared

Quillstack offers three plans: Free, Team, and Business. The Free plan is limited to 3 members and 1 workspace. The Team plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly. The Business plan costs $28 per member per month when billed monthly. Annual billing is charged up front and costs 10 times the monthly price, giving you two months free.

| Feature | Free | Team | Business |
|---------|------|------|----------|
| Members per workspace | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Workspaces | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage per workspace | 500 MB | 50 GB | 500 GB |
| Max file size | 100 MB | 100 MB | 100 MB |
| Page version history | 30 days | 180 days | Unlimited |
| Public page links | No | Yes | Yes |
| API keys per workspace | 1 | 10 | 50 |
| API key expiry | 30 days | 90 days | 365 days |
| SSO (SAML and OIDC) | No | No | Yes |
| Audit log | No | No | Yes (365 days) |
| IP allowlist for API | No | No | Yes |
| Support response time | 3 business days | 1 business day | 4 business hours |

File storage is 500 MB per workspace on Free, 50 GB per workspace on Team, and 500 GB per workspace on Business; the maximum size of a single uploaded file is 100 MB on every plan. If you need more storage, you must upgrade to a higher plan. Storage is not sold separately.

## Features by plan

### Free
The Free plan is ideal for individuals or very small teams who want to try Quillstack without a credit card. You get one workspace, up to 3 members, and 500 MB of storage. Pages, projects, tasks, and the mobile app are all included. Version history is kept for 30 days, and you can create 1 API key that expires after 30 days. Public page links are not available, so you can only share pages with workspace members.

### Team
The Team plan removes member and workspace limits and increases storage to 50 GB per workspace. You can create up to 10 API keys per workspace, and they expire after 90 days instead of 30. Page version history extends to 180 days, and you can share pages publicly with anyone who has the link. The Slack and GitHub integrations are available on Team. Support response time improves to 1 business day.

### Business
The Business plan is built for organizations that need advanced security and compliance features. SSO (SAML 2.0 and OIDC) is available on the Business plan only, and you can enforce SSO-only sign-in to block password-based access. Storage increases to 500 GB per workspace, and page version history is kept without limit. API keys expire after 365 days, you can create up to 50 per workspace, and you can restrict API access by IP address. The audit log retains 365 days of activity and can be exported as CSV. Support response time drops to 4 business hours.

## Troubleshooting

If you try to invite a fourth member on Free, you will see an error prompting you to upgrade. Go to Settings > Billing, choose Team or Business, and add your payment method. The upgrade processes immediately and you can send the invitation right away. You are charged for the new member prorated to the end of your first monthly cycle.

If you are on Team and need SSO, you must upgrade to Business. SSO cannot be added to Team as an add-on. The upgrade takes effect immediately, and you can configure SSO under Settings > Security > Single sign-on as soon as the upgrade completes.
