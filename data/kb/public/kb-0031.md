When you add or remove seats from your Quillstack workspace, the billing adjustment is calculated based on the time remaining in your current billing cycle. Understanding proration helps you predict exactly what you will be charged or credited when your team size changes.

## Before you start

Proration applies only to paid plans (Team and Business). The Free plan is limited to 3 members and does not support additional seats. You must be an Owner or Admin to add or remove seats from Settings > Billing > Seats.

## How proration works for added seats

Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated: you are charged only for the days remaining in the current billing cycle. The charge appears immediately on your payment method. For example, if your Team plan costs $12 per member per month and you add a seat 10 days into a 30-day billing cycle, you pay for the remaining 20 days:

| Days remaining | Full monthly cost | Prorated charge |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 20 out of 30   | $12.00            | $8.00           |
| 10 out of 30   | $12.00            | $4.00           |
| 1 out of 30    | $12.00            | $0.40           |

The new seat is billed at the full monthly rate on every invoice after that, starting with the next billing cycle. Annual subscriptions prorate in the same way, based on days remaining until the annual renewal date.

## How proration works for removed seats

Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice; removed seats are never refunded in cash. The credit is calculated using the same daily rate as an addition. If you remove a Team seat 10 days into a 30-day cycle, the credit is $8.00 and appears as a line item on your next invoice.

The removed member loses access to the workspace immediately. If you remove multiple seats in the same billing cycle, the credits accumulate and all are applied together at the next renewal.

## Proration and plan changes

When you upgrade from Team to Business mid-cycle, the unused portion of your Team subscription becomes a credit and the Business plan is prorated for the remainder of the cycle. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle and no proration occurs. See the upgrade and downgrade article for details.

## Proration on annual billing

Annual billing is charged up front and costs 10 times the monthly price. If you add a seat to an annual subscription, the prorated charge covers only the days until your next annual renewal. For example, adding a Business seat ($28 per month, $280 per year) with 180 days remaining in the year costs approximately $140. Removed seats on annual plans generate credits that reduce your next annual invoice.

## Troubleshooting

**I added a seat but was charged the full monthly amount.** Check the transaction date in Settings > Billing > Invoices. If you added the seat on the first day of your billing cycle, the charge is not prorated because no days have elapsed. The invoice shows the full amount.

**The prorated charge does not match my calculation.** Proration uses the exact number of days in your billing cycle, which varies by month (28 to 31 days). Leap years and month boundaries affect the daily rate. The invoice in Settings > Billing > Invoices shows the exact calculation.

**I removed a seat but see no credit on my latest invoice.** Credits appear on the invoice issued after the removal. If you removed a seat on the last day of a billing cycle, the credit will be small and appears on the next invoice, not the current one.

**Can I get a cash refund instead of a credit for a removed seat?** No. Removed seats always generate account credits, never cash refunds. This policy applies to all plans and billing frequencies.
