Quillstack issues refunds for charges made in error or when you are not satisfied with the service, but refunds are only available for a limited time after the charge. This policy applies to all plans and billing frequencies.

## Before you start

Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge. After 14 days, the charge is final and no refund will be issued. Refunds are never issued automatically or by the support assistant; every refund request is reviewed by the billing team, which replies within 3 business days. To request a refund, you must be an Owner or Admin of the workspace that was charged.

## Steps

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices and find the invoice number for the charge you want refunded (formatted INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN).
2. Contact support from Settings > Support and include the invoice number in your message.
3. Explain why you are requesting a refund (for example, charged after canceling, upgraded by mistake, or dissatisfied with the product).
4. Wait for the billing team to review your request; they reply within 3 business days.
5. If the refund is approved, it appears on your payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

The billing team reviews each request individually and considers factors like the reason for the refund, your account history, and whether you have used the service during the billing period. Approval is not guaranteed, but the team will explain their decision in the reply.

## What is refundable

The table below shows which charges are eligible for refunds and which are not:

| Charge type                          | Refundable within 14 days? | Notes                                                                                   |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Monthly or annual subscription fee   | Yes                        | Prorated if you used the service for part of the billing period.                        |
| Seat additions                       | Yes                        | Prorated based on the time the seat was active.                                         |
| Plan upgrades                        | Yes                        | The upgrade charge can be refunded; the original plan's charge is not affected.         |
| Duplicate charges                    | Yes                        | Reversed automatically after review, no 14-day limit.                                   |
| Seat removals                        | No                         | Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice; removed seats are never refunded in cash. |

If you cancel your subscription within 14 days of a charge and request a refund, the billing team may issue a prorated refund for the unused portion of the billing period. For example, if you cancel 5 days into a monthly cycle, you may receive a refund for the remaining 25 days. This is decided on a case-by-case basis.

## Refunds and account credits

Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice; removed seats are never refunded in cash. This policy applies even if you cancel your subscription before the credit is used. If you cancel and have an unused credit, the credit does not convert to a cash refund. Contact the billing team if you believe you have a special circumstance that warrants an exception.

## Troubleshooting

**I requested a refund 5 days ago and have not received a reply.** The billing team replies within 3 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding holidays). If you submitted your request on a Friday, you may not receive a reply until Wednesday. Check your spam folder for the reply email, which comes from billing@quillstack.com.

**My refund was denied but I think it should have been approved.** The billing team's decision is based on the refund policy and your account history. If you believe the denial was in error, reply to the billing team's email with additional context. They will review your case again, but the second review is final.

**I was refunded but the amount is less than I expected.** Refunds for subscription fees are often prorated based on the number of days you used the service. The billing team's reply email explains the calculation. If the refund amount does not match the explanation, reply to the email and ask for clarification.

**I canceled within 14 days but was told I do not qualify for a refund.** The 14-day window applies to individual charges, not to the entire subscription. If you used the service for the full billing period and then canceled, no refund is issued because you received the service you paid for. Refunds are only issued if you cancel partway through a billing period and request a prorated refund for the unused time.

**Can I get a refund for a charge from last year?** No. Refunds are only possible within 14 days of the charge. After 14 days, the charge is final. If you believe you were charged in error, contact the billing team with your invoice number and they will investigate, but refunds are not guaranteed for old charges.
