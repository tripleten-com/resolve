Quillstack supports SAML 2.0 single sign-on (SSO) on the Business plan, allowing you to manage authentication through your identity provider. You configure SAML by setting three values in Quillstack and providing your identity provider with the ACS URL. After SAML is configured, you can optionally enforce SSO-only sign-in to block password-based sign-in for everyone except workspace Owners.

## Before you start

You must be on the Business plan and have Owner or Admin permissions in the workspace. You will need access to your identity provider's SAML configuration interface, such as Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, or OneLogin. SSO is available only on Business; Team and Free plans do not support SAML or OIDC.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your identity provider and create a new SAML application for Quillstack.
2. Set the ACS URL (also called the reply URL or callback URL) in your identity provider to `https://app.quillstack.com/sso/saml/acs`.
3. Copy the entity ID, sign-in URL, and X.509 certificate from your identity provider.
4. Sign in to Quillstack at https://app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Security > Single sign-on.
5. Under **SAML configuration**, paste the values you copied into the corresponding fields: `sso.entity_id`, `sso.sign_in_url`, and `sso.certificate`.
6. Click **Save SAML settings**.
7. Click **Test SAML connection** to verify the configuration. You will be redirected to your identity provider to sign in, then returned to Quillstack if the test succeeds.
8. If the test succeeds, SAML is now active and members can sign in through your identity provider.

## SAML settings reference

```
sso.entity_id: The entity ID or issuer URL from your identity provider
sso.sign_in_url: The SSO sign-in URL from your identity provider
sso.certificate: The X.509 certificate (PEM format, including BEGIN and END lines)
```

After SAML is configured, you can optionally set `sso.enforce` to `true` under Settings > Security > Single sign-on > Enforce SSO-only sign-in. This blocks password sign-in for everyone except Owners, who keep password sign-in as a recovery path in case the identity provider is unavailable.

## Troubleshooting

**The test connection fails with a certificate error.** Make sure you copied the entire X.509 certificate from your identity provider, including the `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----` and `-----END CERTIFICATE-----` lines. The certificate must be in PEM format with no extra spaces or line breaks.

**Members cannot sign in after enabling SAML.** Verify that the `sso.sign_in_url` is correct and that the SAML application is assigned to the users who need access in your identity provider. If you enabled `sso.enforce`, members must sign in through the identity provider and cannot use their Quillstack password.

**I am locked out after enabling sso.enforce.** If you are a workspace Owner, you can still sign in with your Quillstack password at https://app.quillstack.com/sign-in. If you are an Admin or another role, contact a workspace Owner to sign in and disable `sso.enforce` or fix the SAML configuration.

| Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| sso.entity_id | Identifies your identity provider to Quillstack. |
| sso.sign_in_url | The URL where Quillstack redirects users to sign in via SAML. |
| sso.certificate | The public key used to verify SAML assertions from your identity provider. |
| sso.enforce | When true, blocks password sign-in for everyone except Owners. |
