If you lose access to your authenticator app or phone, you can sign in to Quillstack with one of your recovery codes. Each recovery code works only once, so keep them in a safe place. After you sign in, you can disable two-factor authentication or set it up on a new device.

## Before you start

Recovery codes are issued when you first enable two-factor authentication. Quillstack generates 10 single-use codes, and you should save them securely—in a password manager, encrypted file, or printed document stored offline. If you did not save your recovery codes and cannot access your authenticator app, you will need to contact support to regain access to your account.

Two-factor authentication is available on every plan. Each recovery code is alphanumeric, typically 8 to 10 characters, and is shown only once during setup.

## Steps

### Sign in with a recovery code

1. Go to https://app.quillstack.com and enter your email and password.
2. On the two-factor authentication screen, select **Use a recovery code**.
3. Enter one of your recovery codes exactly as it was issued (codes are case-sensitive).
4. Click **Verify**.

You are now signed in. The recovery code you used is consumed and cannot be used again. If you have remaining codes, they are still valid for future sign-ins.

### Disable or reconfigure 2FA after signing in

Once you are signed in with a recovery code, you have two options: disable two-factor authentication entirely or set it up on a new device.

To disable 2FA:

1. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication**.
2. Click **Disable 2FA**.
3. Confirm your password.

Two-factor authentication is now turned off. You can re-enable it at any time, and Quillstack will issue a new set of 10 recovery codes.

To set up 2FA on a new device:

1. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication**.
2. Click **Reconfigure 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with your new authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, or any TOTP-compatible app).
4. Enter the six-digit code from the app to confirm.
5. Save the new set of 10 recovery codes that Quillstack displays.

Your old recovery codes are no longer valid after reconfiguration. Always save the new codes securely.

## Troubleshooting

**I did not save my recovery codes and cannot access my authenticator app.**  
Contact support at support@quillstack.com from the email address associated with your Quillstack account. Include your workspace name and the email you use to sign in. Support will verify your identity and disable two-factor authentication on your account so you can sign in with your password. Response times vary by plan: 3 business days on Free, 1 business day on Team, and 4 business hours on Business.

**My recovery code is rejected as invalid.**  
Recovery codes are case-sensitive and must be entered exactly as shown when they were issued. Check for leading or trailing spaces if you copied the code from a document. If you have already used the code in a previous sign-in, it is consumed and will not work again. Try another code from your saved list.

**I ran out of recovery codes.**  
Each set includes 10 codes, and each code works only once. If you have used all 10, you must contact support to regain access. After support disables 2FA on your account, sign in with your password, then re-enable two-factor authentication to receive a new set of codes.

**Can I generate new recovery codes without disabling 2FA?**  
No. The only way to receive new recovery codes is to reconfigure two-factor authentication. This invalidates your old codes and issues a fresh set of 10. If you want to refresh your codes but keep the same authenticator app, you can reconfigure 2FA using the same device.
