A workspace is a shared environment where your team creates pages, manages projects, and tracks tasks together. Every Quillstack account starts with one workspace, and you can create additional workspaces if you upgrade from the Free plan. This article shows you how to create a workspace and configure its basic settings.

## Before you start

You need a Quillstack account to create a workspace. Sign up at app.quillstack.com if you have not already done so. The Free plan is limited to 3 members and 1 workspace, so if you already have a workspace on Free, you must upgrade to Team or Business before creating another one. When you create a workspace, you become its Owner automatically. Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer; only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and click your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Select **Create workspace** from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter a workspace name. Choose something descriptive, like your team name or project name. You can change this later under Settings > General.
4. Choose a workspace URL slug. This appears in the address bar when you view pages in this workspace, for example app.quillstack.com/w/your-slug. The slug must be unique across all Quillstack workspaces.
5. Select a time zone. This controls when daily email digests are sent and how timestamps appear in the audit log. You can change it later under Settings > General.
6. Click **Create workspace**. You are taken to the new workspace immediately.

After you create the workspace, invite team members from Settings > Members and start creating pages or projects. If you are on the Free plan and try to create a second workspace, you will see an error message prompting you to upgrade. The upgrade takes effect immediately and you can then create additional workspaces without limits.

## Workspace settings

| Setting | Location | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Workspace name and slug | Settings > General | The slug cannot be changed after creation |
| Time zone | Settings > General | Affects digest timing and audit log timestamps |
| Default page permissions | Settings > Permissions | Controls who can view new pages by default |
| Member roles | Settings > Members | Owner, Admin, Editor, or Viewer |

The workspace Owner is set during creation and can only be changed by the current Owner under Settings > Members > Transfer ownership. If you need to delete the workspace, only the Owner can do so from Settings > General > Delete workspace. Deleted workspaces are retained for 14 days and can be restored by the Owner before permanent deletion.

## Troubleshooting

If you see "Workspace limit reached" when trying to create a workspace, you are on the Free plan and already have one workspace. Upgrade to Team or Business from Settings > Billing in your existing workspace, then return to the workspace creation flow. The upgrade processes immediately and you can create the new workspace right away.

If your chosen workspace slug is already taken, try adding your company name or a number to make it unique. The slug cannot be changed after creation, so choose carefully. If you create a workspace and then realize you need a different slug, you must delete the workspace and create a new one with the correct slug within the 14-day retention window.
