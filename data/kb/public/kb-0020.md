Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of security to your Quillstack account by requiring a time-based code from your phone in addition to your password. You can enable 2FA from your account settings using any authenticator app that supports TOTP, such as Authy, Google Authenticator, or 1Password. When you turn on 2FA, Quillstack generates 10 single-use recovery codes that let you sign in if you lose access to your authenticator device.

## Before you start

You need an authenticator app installed on your phone or another device. Popular options include Authy, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and 1Password. Make sure you can open the app and scan a QR code or manually enter a setup key before you begin.

Two-factor authentication is available on every Quillstack plan: Free, Team, and Business. You enable it for your own account, and it does not affect other members of your workspace unless an Admin or Owner has turned on the workspace-wide requirement under Settings > Security > Require 2FA.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Quillstack at app.quillstack.com and click your avatar in the top right corner.
2. Select **Account settings** from the dropdown menu.
3. Go to the **Security** tab.
4. Click **Enable two-factor authentication**.
5. Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code displayed on the screen. If you cannot scan the code, click **Enter key manually** and type the text key into your app.
6. Your authenticator app will display a six-digit code that refreshes every 30 seconds. Enter the current code in the **Verification code** field and click **Verify and enable**.
7. Quillstack will display 10 recovery codes. Copy these codes and store them in a safe place, such as a password manager or a secure note. Each code can be used only once.
8. Check the box confirming you have saved your recovery codes, then click **Done**.

Your account now requires both your password and a code from your authenticator app every time you sign in. Web sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity, and mobile app sessions expire after 90 days, so you will be prompted for a new code when your session ends.

## Recovery codes

Recovery codes let you sign in if you lose your phone, uninstall your authenticator app, or get a new device. Each of the 10 codes works only once. After you use a recovery code to sign in, you should disable 2FA and re-enable it with your new authenticator device, which will issue a fresh set of 10 codes.

To view your remaining recovery codes or generate a new set, go to Account settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and click **View recovery codes**. Generating new codes immediately invalidates all previous codes.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Use a recovery code to sign in | That code becomes invalid; 9 codes remain |
| Generate new recovery codes | All 10 old codes become invalid; 10 new codes are issued |
| Disable and re-enable 2FA | All old codes become invalid; 10 new codes are issued |

## Troubleshooting

**The code from my authenticator app is rejected.** Make sure the time on your phone is set to automatic. TOTP codes are time-based, and even a few minutes of clock drift will cause codes to fail. If the time is correct and codes still do not work, try entering the next code that appears after the current one refreshes.

**I lost my phone and my recovery codes.** Contact support at support@quillstack.com from the email address on your account. Support will verify your identity and disable 2FA so you can sign in with your password. You should re-enable 2FA immediately after signing in.

**I enabled 2FA but my session did not ask for a code.** You are still signed in from before you enabled 2FA. Sign out and sign in again to see the 2FA prompt.

**Can I use SMS or email for 2FA instead of an app?** No. Quillstack supports only TOTP authenticator apps. SMS and email are less secure and are not offered.
