The billing contact is the email address that receives invoices, payment receipts, and payment failure notifications. By default, the workspace Owner is the billing contact, but you can change it to any email address—it does not have to belong to a workspace member. Only the Owner can update the billing contact.

## Before you start

You must be the workspace Owner to change the billing contact. Admins can view billing details under Settings > Billing, but they cannot change the billing contact. Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer; only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact. If you need to change the billing contact and you are not the Owner, ask the current Owner to make the change or transfer ownership to you first.

## When to change the billing contact

You might change the billing contact when:

- The current contact left the company or changed roles.
- You want invoices sent to a shared billing or accounting email address.
- The workspace Owner does not handle billing and wants someone else to receive invoice notifications.
- You transferred ownership and want the new Owner to receive invoices.

Changing the billing contact does not affect who can view or manage billing settings. Any Owner or Admin can still access Settings > Billing and update the payment method, upgrade the plan, or download invoices.

## Steps

### Change the billing contact

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Billing contact.
2. Enter the new email address.
3. Click Save.
4. The new contact receives a confirmation email immediately.

All future invoices and billing notifications are sent to the new address. Invoices are numbered INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN (for example INV-202608-00417), issued on the first day of each billing cycle, emailed to the billing contact, and available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Past invoices are not resent to the new contact, but they remain available for download under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## What the new billing contact receives

Once you update the billing contact, that email address receives:

| Email type | When it is sent |
|------------|-----------------|
| Invoice | First day of each billing cycle |
| Payment receipt | Immediately after a successful charge |
| Payment failure | When a charge fails, and again on each retry |
| Upcoming renewal | 7 days before the next billing cycle |
| Seat change confirmation | When seats are added or removed |

The billing contact does not need a Quillstack account to receive these emails. They can download invoices from the links in the email without signing in.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot find the billing contact setting.** You must be the workspace Owner. If you are an Admin, you can view billing details but you cannot change the billing contact. Ask the Owner to make the change or transfer ownership to you.

**The new billing contact did not receive the confirmation email.** Check the spam folder. If it is not there, verify you entered the email address correctly under Settings > Billing > Billing contact. You can change it again if you made a typo.

**I changed the billing contact but I still receive invoices at the old address.** It can take up to 10 minutes for the change to propagate. If you still receive an invoice at the old address after that, contact support with the invoice number and the new billing contact email.

**Can I set multiple billing contacts?** No. Quillstack supports one billing contact per workspace. If you need multiple people to receive invoices, set the billing contact to a shared email address or forward invoices manually after you receive them.

**I am the billing contact but I cannot update the payment method.** The billing contact email address does not grant any permissions in the workspace. To update the payment method, you must sign in as a workspace Owner or Admin and go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.
