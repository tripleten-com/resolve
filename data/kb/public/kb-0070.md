Quillstack retains deleted content and historical data for recovery and compliance purposes. This article explains how long each type of data is kept and when it is permanently deleted, covering pages, workspaces, version history, and audit logs.

## Before you start

Retention periods vary by content type and plan. Deleted pages stay in Trash for 30 days before permanent deletion. Deleted workspaces are retained for 14 days. Page version history is kept for 30 days on Free, 180 days on Team, and without limit on Business. The audit log is Business-only and retained for 365 days.

## Deleted pages

Deleted pages move to Trash and can be restored by any Editor, Admin, or Owner. After 30 days in Trash, pages are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

1. Go to **Pages > Trash** to view all deleted pages in the workspace.
2. Click a page title to preview its content before restoring.
3. Click **Restore** to move the page back to its original location.
4. Pages that have been in Trash for more than 30 days are removed automatically and do not appear in the Trash list.

All file attachments associated with a deleted page are also deleted when the page is permanently removed after 30 days.

## Deleted workspaces

A deleted workspace is retained for 14 days and can be restored by an Owner from Settings > Workspaces > Deleted. After 14 days it is permanently deleted.

1. Sign in at app.quillstack.com with the email address associated with the deleted workspace.
2. Go to **Settings > Workspaces > Deleted**.
3. Click **Restore** next to the workspace name.
4. All pages, projects, tasks, and members are restored exactly as they were at the time of deletion.

After 14 days, the workspace and all of its data—pages, tasks, projects, files, and version history—are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Page version history

Page version history is kept for 30 days on Free, 180 days on Team, and without limit on Business. Versions older than the retention limit are automatically deleted.

1. Open any page and click the clock icon in the top-right corner to view **Version history**.
2. Select a version from the timeline to preview its content.
3. Click **Restore this version** to revert the page to that snapshot.
4. On Free and Team, versions older than the retention limit disappear from the timeline and cannot be restored.

Business workspaces keep version history indefinitely. If you downgrade from Business to Team, versions older than 180 days are deleted within 7 days of the downgrade.

## Audit log

The audit log is Business-only, retained for 365 days, and exportable as CSV from Settings > Security > Audit log. Events older than 365 days are automatically deleted and do not appear in exports.

Each log entry records the user, action, timestamp, and affected resource (page, task, project, or workspace setting). If you downgrade from Business to Team or Free, the audit log becomes read-only immediately and all events are deleted after 30 days.

## Data retention summary

| Data type | Retention period | Restoration method |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| Deleted pages | 30 days | Pages > Trash |
| Deleted workspaces | 14 days | Settings > Workspaces > Deleted |
| Page version history (Free) | 30 days | Version history on the page |
| Page version history (Team) | 180 days | Version history on the page |
| Page version history (Business) | Unlimited | Version history on the page |
| Audit log (Business) | 365 days | Settings > Security > Audit log |

## Permanent deletion requests

If you need to permanently delete a workspace or specific content before the standard retention period expires, contact support with the workspace name and resource details. The billing team reviews each request and confirms deletion within 3 business days.
