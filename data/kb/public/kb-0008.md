Only a workspace Owner can delete a workspace or restore one that has been deleted. Deleted workspaces are kept for 14 days and can be restored during that window. After 14 days, the workspace and all of its data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Before you start

You must be the Owner of the workspace to delete it or restore it. Admins, Editors, and Viewers cannot perform these actions. If you are not sure of your role, check under Settings > Members. If you need to delete a workspace but you are not the Owner, ask the current Owner to transfer ownership to you first or to delete the workspace on your behalf.

Deleting a workspace removes access for every member immediately. All pages, projects, tasks, files, and settings are hidden but retained for 14 days. During this time, the workspace does not appear in your workspace list and does not count toward your plan limits.

## Steps

### Delete a workspace

1. Open the workspace you want to delete and go to **Settings > Workspaces**.
2. Click the **Delete workspace** button at the bottom of the page.
3. Enter the workspace name exactly as shown to confirm the deletion.
4. Click **Delete permanently** to move the workspace to deleted status.

The workspace is removed from your workspace list immediately. You and every other member lose access, but the data is retained for 14 days in case you need to restore it.

### Restore a deleted workspace

1. Sign in to Quillstack and go to **Settings > Workspaces > Deleted**.
2. Find the workspace you want to restore in the list of deleted workspaces.
3. Click **Restore** next to the workspace name.
4. The workspace reappears in your workspace list and all members regain access immediately.

Restored workspaces include all pages, projects, tasks, files, members, and settings exactly as they were at the time of deletion. Billing resumes on the same plan and payment method.

## Limits

| Retention period | Who can delete | Who can restore |
|------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| 14 days          | Owner only     | Owner only      |

After 14 days, the workspace and all of its data are permanently deleted from Quillstack's systems. This includes all pages, projects, tasks, uploaded files, API keys, integrations, and audit logs. Permanent deletion cannot be undone.

## Troubleshooting

**I am not the Owner and cannot delete the workspace.** Ask the current Owner to delete the workspace or to transfer ownership to you first. You can see who the Owner is under Settings > Members.

**I deleted a workspace by mistake and the 14 days have passed.** After 14 days, the workspace is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. If you have a recent export, you can create a new workspace and import the data manually.

**The workspace I deleted is not in Settings > Workspaces > Deleted.** Only the Owner who deleted the workspace can see it in the deleted list. If another Owner deleted it, sign in with that account to restore it, or ask them to restore it for you.

**What happens to billing when I delete a workspace?** Deleting a workspace does not cancel your subscription or issue a refund. If you want to stop billing, go to Settings > Billing > Subscription and cancel before deleting the workspace. Restoring a deleted workspace resumes billing on the same plan.
