Quillstack keeps you signed in so you do not have to enter your password every time you open the app. Web sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity, and mobile app sessions expire after 90 days. You can also sign out manually from one device or from every device at once if you need to end all sessions immediately.

## Before you start

A session begins when you sign in and ends when you sign out, when it expires due to inactivity, or when you revoke all sessions from your account settings. Closing the browser or app does not end the session. If you share a computer or device with someone else, always sign out manually rather than just closing the window.

Two-factor authentication and SSO do not change session expiry. Once you sign in, the session lasts for the same duration regardless of the authentication method you used.

## Session expiry

Web sessions at app.quillstack.com expire after 30 days of inactivity. If you open Quillstack at least once every 30 days, the session renews and you stay signed in. Mobile app sessions on iOS and Android expire after 90 days of inactivity. The longer duration on mobile accounts for devices you may not use every day.

When a session expires, you are redirected to the sign-in page the next time you open Quillstack. You will need to enter your password (or sign in through SSO) and complete two-factor authentication if you have it enabled. Expired sessions are deleted automatically and cannot be restored.

If your account is locked after too many failed sign-in attempts, all sessions remain active and the lock applies only to new sign-ins. Existing sessions continue to work until they expire or you sign out.

## Steps: sign out from one device

1. Open Quillstack on the device you want to sign out from.
2. Click your profile picture in the top right corner and select Sign out.
3. You are signed out immediately and redirected to the sign-in page.

This ends only the session on the current device. Other devices where you are signed in stay signed in.

## Steps: sign out from all devices

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Account > Security.
2. Scroll to the Active sessions section and click Sign out everywhere.
3. Confirm the action in the dialog.
4. Every session on every device is ended immediately, including the one you are using. You are redirected to the sign-in page.

Use this option if you lose a device, suspect your account has been accessed without permission, or want to ensure no one else has access. After you sign out everywhere, you will need to sign in again on every device you use.

## Session activity table

| Device type | Expiry after inactivity | Renewed by |
|-------------|-------------------------|------------|
| Web browser | 30 days | Any page load at app.quillstack.com |
| Mobile app (iOS 16+, Android 11+) | 90 days | Opening the app while online |

## Troubleshooting

If you are signed out unexpectedly and you know you have used Quillstack recently, check whether someone with access to your account clicked "Sign out everywhere" under Settings > Account > Security. That action ends all sessions without warning. If you did not do this yourself and no one else should have access, change your password immediately and enable two-factor authentication.

If the sign-in page says your session expired but you used Quillstack yesterday, verify that you are going to app.quillstack.com and not a different URL. Sessions are tied to the domain, and bookmarks or links to old staging environments will not work.

If you sign out from one device but find yourself still signed in when you return to it later, check whether your browser or password manager is automatically signing you back in. Some password managers offer to fill credentials and submit the sign-in form without asking. Disable autofill for Quillstack if you want manual sign-out to persist.
