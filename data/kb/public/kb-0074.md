Quillstack support is available by email for all plans. This article explains how to contact support, what information to include in your request, and how quickly you can expect a response based on your plan.

## Before you start

First-response targets for support are 3 business days on Free, 1 business day on Team, and 4 business hours on Business. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays in the United States. Response times are targets, not guarantees, and actual replies may arrive sooner.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings > Help > Contact support** at app.quillstack.com or send an email directly to support@quillstack.com.
2. Include your workspace name and the email address associated with your account in the message.
3. Describe the issue in detail: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and any error codes or messages you saw.
4. Attach screenshots, API request logs, or exported diagnostic files if they help illustrate the problem.
5. If the issue involves billing, include the invoice number (format: INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN) or the date and amount of the charge.

You will receive an automated acknowledgment immediately. A support agent replies within the target time for your plan: 3 business days on Free, 1 business day on Team, or 4 business hours on Business.

## What to include in your request

The more context you provide, the faster support can investigate. Include the following details when you contact us:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Workspace name | The name of your workspace (visible at the top of the sidebar) |
| Account email | The email address you use to sign in |
| Plan | Free, Team, or Business (if you know it) |
| Error code | Any error code shown (for example QS-1042, QS-1099) |
| Steps to reproduce | Numbered list of what you did before the issue occurred |
| Expected behavior | What you expected to happen |
| Actual behavior | What actually happened |
| Browser or device | For example Chrome 120 on macOS, or Android 13 on Pixel 7 |
| Timestamp | When the issue occurred (include your time zone) |

For API issues, include the full HTTP request (curl command or raw headers and body) and the response, including the request_id field if present.

## Response time targets

Response times vary by plan and are measured from the moment your email arrives to the first reply from a support agent:

| Plan | First-response target | Coverage |
|------|----------------------|----------|
| Free | 3 business days | Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm US Eastern |
| Team | 1 business day | Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm US Eastern |
| Business | 4 business hours | Monday–Friday, 9am–9pm US Eastern |

If your issue is urgent and you are on the Business plan, include "URGENT" in the subject line. Business customers with urgent issues (for example workspace inaccessible or data loss) receive priority routing to senior support engineers.

## Billing and account access

Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged. If you contact support about a refund, duplicate charge, or invoice question, we verify your role before sharing any billing information. For details, see the policy in the article "Who can support discuss billing with?"

Support cannot reset your password or disable two-factor authentication without verifying your identity. If you are locked out of your account, use the self-service password reset at app.quillstack.com/reset or sign in with a recovery code if you have 2FA enabled.

## Escalation and follow-up

After the first response, support continues the conversation until the issue is resolved or a workaround is provided. If the issue requires engineering investigation, support creates an internal ticket and updates you every 3 business days with progress. You can reply to any support email to add more information or ask follow-up questions, and the thread remains linked to the original ticket.

If you are not satisfied with the resolution, ask to escalate the ticket to a senior support engineer or manager. Escalations are reviewed within 1 business day on Team and Business, or 3 business days on Free.

## Known issues and service status

Before contacting support, check the known-issues page at https://quillstack.com/status for ongoing incidents or planned maintenance. If the issue you are experiencing is listed there, support is already investigating and you will receive an update when the issue is resolved. You can subscribe to status updates by email or RSS at the bottom of the status page.
