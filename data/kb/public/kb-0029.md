Quillstack invoices show the charges for your subscription, including the plan, number of seats, billing period, and any prorated adjustments. Every invoice is numbered, dated, and sent to your billing contact on the first day of each billing cycle. You can view and download invoices at any time from your workspace settings.

## Before you start

Invoices are issued to the workspace, not to individual members. Only the workspace Owner and Admins can view invoices under Settings > Billing > Invoices. If you are an Editor or Viewer, you will not see the Billing section.

The billing contact receives every invoice by email. If you are not receiving invoices, check that the correct address is set under Settings > Billing > Billing contact. The billing contact does not need to be a member of the workspace, so you can use a shared email like billing@yourcompany.com.

Invoices are numbered in the format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN, where YYYYMM is the year and month and NNNNN is a sequential number. For example, INV-202608-00417 is invoice number 417 issued in August 2026. This format makes it easy to file and reference invoices in your accounting system.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com as an Owner or Admin and go to Settings > Billing > Invoices.
2. Find the invoice you want to review in the list. Invoices are sorted by date, with the most recent at the top.
3. Click the invoice number to open the full invoice in a new tab.
4. Review the details: invoice number, issue date, billing period, line items, subtotal, tax, and total.

The invoice shows the plan name (Team or Business), the number of seats, and the per-seat price. The Team plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly, and the Business plan costs $28 per member per month when billed monthly. Annual billing is charged up front at 10 times the monthly price, giving you two months free.

## Invoice line items

Each invoice contains one or more line items. A typical monthly invoice has one line for the subscription (plan name, number of seats, and total). If you added or removed seats during the billing cycle, you will see additional lines for prorated charges or credits.

Adding a seat mid-cycle creates a prorated charge for the remaining days in the cycle. For example, if you add a seat on day 15 of a 30-day cycle, you are charged 50% of the monthly per-seat price for that seat. The prorated charge appears as a separate line item on the next invoice.

Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice. The credit amount is also prorated based on the remaining days in the cycle. Credits are never refunded in cash; they always reduce the amount due on a future invoice.

## Troubleshooting

If an invoice shows a charge you do not recognize, check the line item description for the billing period and number of seats. Most unexpected charges are prorated adjustments from adding seats mid-cycle. If you still cannot identify the charge, contact support with the invoice number and they will explain each line item.

If the invoice total does not match the amount charged to your card, verify that you are looking at the correct invoice. Invoices are issued on the first day of the billing cycle, but the charge may process a few hours later. Also check for any credits from removed seats that reduced the amount due.

If your VAT or tax ID does not appear on the invoice, it was added after the invoice was issued. Tax IDs saved under Settings > Billing > Tax details appear only on invoices issued after you save them. Earlier invoices cannot be reissued with a tax ID; you will need to request a credit note or amended invoice from your tax authority if required.
