Quillstack helps teams document their work and track projects in one place. You can create pages, organize them into workspaces, build task lists, and invite your team to collaborate. This guide walks you through the basics so you can start working in Quillstack today.

## Before you start

Sign up for a free account at app.quillstack.com. You will create your first workspace during sign-up, and you become its Owner automatically. Quillstack offers three plans: Free, Team, and Business. New accounts start on the Free plan, which is limited to 3 members and 1 workspace. You can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and confirm you see your workspace name in the top left corner.
2. Create your first page by clicking **New page** in the sidebar or pressing `Cmd+N` (Mac) or `Ctrl+N` (Windows).
3. Give your page a title and start writing. Pages support Markdown formatting, headings, lists, tables, and inline images.
4. Upload a file by dragging it onto the page or clicking the attachment icon in the toolbar.
5. Organize pages into folders by dragging them in the sidebar or clicking the **···** menu next to a page and choosing **Move to folder**.
6. Create a project by clicking **Projects** in the sidebar, then **New project**. Give it a name and add your first task.
7. Invite team members from Settings > Members > **Invite member**. Enter their email address and choose a role: Viewer, Editor, Admin, or Owner.

## What to do next

| Action | Where to go |
|--------|-------------|
| Learn about workspace roles and permissions | Settings > Members, or see the roles guide |
| Explore project boards and task tracking | Projects > any project > Board view |
| Share a page with someone outside your workspace | Open the page, click Share (Team or Business only) |
| Set up integrations with Slack or GitHub | Settings > Integrations |

Once you have created a few pages and invited your team, you can set up projects to track work, configure notifications under Settings > Notifications, and explore templates for common page types like meeting notes or project briefs. The Free plan gives you everything you need to get started, and you can upgrade when you need more storage, additional workspaces, or advanced features like SSO.

## Troubleshooting

If you do not see the **New page** button, check that you are signed in and that your role is Editor, Admin, or Owner. Viewers can read pages but cannot create or edit them. If you cannot invite a third member on the Free plan, you have reached the 3-member limit; upgrade to Team or Business to add more people.

If your workspace name does not appear in the top left, you may not have completed sign-up. Check your email for a verification link and click it to activate your account. If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support from the sign-in page.
