Pages are the foundation of your workspace, where you write documentation, meeting notes, project briefs, and any other content your team needs to share. You can organize pages into folders, add attachments, and format text with Markdown. This guide covers creating pages, organizing them, and managing attachments.

## Before you start

You must be an Editor, Admin, or Owner to create or edit pages. Viewers can read pages but cannot create or modify them. Check your role under Settings > Members. Pages you create are visible to everyone in the workspace by default, but you can change permissions for individual pages under Share > Workspace members. File storage is 500 MB per workspace on Free, 50 GB per workspace on Team, and 500 GB per workspace on Business; the maximum size of a single uploaded file is 100 MB on every plan.

## Steps

### Create a page

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open your workspace.
2. Click **New page** in the sidebar or press `Cmd+N` (Mac) or `Ctrl+N` (Windows).
3. Enter a title at the top of the page. Titles are required and appear in the sidebar and search results.
4. Write your content in the editor. Quillstack supports Markdown syntax for headings, lists, links, bold, italic, code blocks, and tables.
5. To add an attachment, drag a file onto the page or click the attachment icon in the toolbar and choose a file. Supported formats include images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and documents.
6. Click **Publish** or press `Cmd+S` (Mac) or `Ctrl+S` (Windows) to save the page. It appears in the sidebar immediately.

### Organize pages into folders

1. In the sidebar, click the **···** menu next to a page and select **Move to folder**.
2. Choose an existing folder from the list or click **New folder** to create one.
3. Enter a folder name and click **Create**. The page moves into the folder and the sidebar updates.
4. To move a page by dragging, click and hold the page name in the sidebar, then drag it onto a folder. Release to drop it into the folder.
5. To create a nested folder, drag one folder onto another. You can nest folders up to 5 levels deep.

## Storage and file limits

| Plan | Storage per workspace | Max file size | Total workspaces |
|------|-----------------------|---------------|------------------|
| Free | 500 MB | 100 MB | 1 |
| Team | 50 GB | 100 MB | Unlimited |
| Business | 500 GB | 100 MB | Unlimited |

If you reach your storage limit, you must delete files or upgrade to a higher plan. Storage is calculated across all pages, projects, and attachments in the workspace. To check your current usage, go to Settings > General and look for the storage meter at the top of the page. Deleted pages stay in Trash for 30 days and still count toward your storage limit until they are permanently deleted.

## Troubleshooting

If you see "File too large" when uploading an attachment, the file exceeds 100 MB. Compress the file, split it into smaller parts, or upload it to an external service and link to it from your page. The 100 MB limit applies on every plan and cannot be increased.

If the **New page** button is grayed out, your role is Viewer. Ask an Admin or Owner to change your role to Editor under Settings > Members. If you are an Editor and the button is still grayed out, your workspace may be in read-only mode due to a failed payment. Check Settings > Billing for alerts.

If you reach your storage limit on Free, you cannot upload more files until you delete existing attachments or upgrade to Team. Deleted pages stay in Trash for 30 days and can be restored by any Editor, Admin, or Owner; after 30 days they are permanently deleted. Deleting a page from Trash frees up its storage immediately.
