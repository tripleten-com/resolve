When you delete a page in Quillstack, it moves to Trash rather than disappearing immediately. Pages stay in Trash for 30 days, giving you time to recover them if you deleted something by mistake. Any Editor, Admin, or Owner can restore a page during this window.

## Before you start

You need Editor, Admin, or Owner permissions in the workspace to restore deleted pages. Viewers cannot access Trash or restore pages. If you are not sure of your role, check under Settings > Members.

Pages remain in Trash for exactly 30 days from the moment of deletion. After that, they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. If you need a page that was deleted more than 30 days ago, you will need to recreate it from scratch or restore it from a workspace export if you have one.

## Steps

1. Click **Pages** in the sidebar, then click **Trash** at the bottom of the page list.
2. Find the page you want to restore using the search box or by scrolling through the list.
3. Hover over the page title and click the **Restore** button that appears on the right.
4. The page returns to its original location in your workspace and is immediately available to everyone who had access before it was deleted.

If you deleted multiple pages and want to restore all of them, repeat these steps for each page. There is no bulk restore option at this time.

## Limits

| Plan       | Trash retention | Who can restore |
|------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Free       | 30 days         | Editor, Admin, Owner |
| Team       | 30 days         | Editor, Admin, Owner |
| Business   | 30 days         | Editor, Admin, Owner |

All plans follow the same 30-day retention rule. After 30 days, deleted pages are removed from Trash and permanently deleted from Quillstack's systems.

## Troubleshooting

**I do not see the Trash link.** Only Editors, Admins, and Owners can access Trash. If you are a Viewer, ask a workspace member with higher permissions to restore the page for you.

**The page I need is not in Trash.** If the page was deleted more than 30 days ago, it has been permanently deleted and cannot be restored. Check whether you have a recent workspace export that includes the page, and recreate it manually if needed.

**I restored a page but it is empty.** Page restorations include all content, attachments, and version history that existed at the time of deletion. If the page appears empty, it may have been empty when it was deleted. Check the version history to see earlier states of the page.

**Can I permanently delete a page from Trash before 30 days?** No. Once a page is in Trash, it stays there for the full 30 days. If you need to remove sensitive content immediately, contact support and provide the page URL and workspace ID.
