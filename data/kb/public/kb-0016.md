Quillstack workspaces use four roles to control what members can see and do. Each role has a specific set of permissions, and only the Owner can perform certain critical actions like deleting the workspace or changing who receives invoices. Understanding these roles helps you assign the right level of access to each team member.

## Before you start

Every workspace has exactly one Owner. The Owner is the person who created the workspace or the person to whom ownership was transferred. You can have as many Admins, Editors, and Viewers as your plan allows. The Free plan is limited to 3 members total, while Team and Business plans have no member limit.

## Roles and permissions

Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer. Here is what each role can do:

| Permission | Viewer | Editor | Admin | Owner |
|------------|--------|--------|-------|-------|
| View pages and tasks | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit pages | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit tasks | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Delete pages and tasks | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Invite members | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove members | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Change member roles | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Archive and restore projects | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage integrations | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| View and export audit log | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Change workspace settings | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Delete the workspace | No | No | No | Yes |
| Transfer ownership | No | No | No | Yes |
| Change the billing contact | No | No | No | Yes |

Only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact. Admins can do almost everything else, including managing members and configuring security settings. Editors can create and edit content but cannot manage people or settings. Viewers can see everything but cannot make changes.

## Steps

### Check your role

1. Go to Settings > Members.
2. Find your name in the member list.
3. Your role appears in the Role column next to your name.

If you are the Owner, you will also see a badge next to your name in the top-right corner of the app.

### Change someone's role

1. Go to Settings > Members.
2. Find the person whose role you want to change.
3. Click the dropdown in the Role column and select the new role.
4. The change takes effect immediately.

You need Admin or Owner permissions to change roles. You cannot change your own role, and you cannot change the Owner's role. To transfer ownership, the current Owner must follow the transfer process.

## Owner-only actions

The Owner has three permissions that no one else has, even Admins. These are intentionally restricted because they have permanent or financial consequences:

- **Delete the workspace.** This removes all data after a 14-day retention period and cannot be undone after that window closes.
- **Transfer ownership.** This reassigns the Owner role to another member and demotes the current Owner to Admin.
- **Change the billing contact.** This updates the email address that receives invoices and payment failure notifications.

If you need to perform one of these actions and you are not the Owner, ask the current Owner to either do it for you or transfer ownership to you first.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot invite a new member.** You need Admin or Owner permissions to invite members. If you are an Editor or Viewer, ask an Admin or the Owner to send the invitation.

**I want to remove someone but the option is grayed out.** You cannot remove the Owner. If you need to remove the person who is currently the Owner, ask them to transfer ownership first, then remove them.

**An Admin changed workspace settings and I want to undo it.** Admins have the same permissions as the Owner for most settings. If you are the Owner and want to restrict this, you can demote the person to Editor or Viewer, but there is no way to limit an Admin's access to specific settings.

**I am the Owner but I cannot see billing details.** The billing contact receives invoices, but any Owner or Admin can view billing details under Settings > Billing. If you cannot access that page, check that you are signed in with the correct account.
