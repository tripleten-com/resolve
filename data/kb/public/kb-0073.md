Quillstack staff have access to certain account data to provide support and maintain the platform. This article explains what support and engineering teams can and cannot see, how your data is protected, and what information we never ask for.

## Before you start

Quillstack staff can never see a customer's password and support never asks for it. Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged, and never shares information about another customer's workspace. All staff access to customer data is logged in internal audit systems and reviewed regularly.

## What staff can see

Support and engineering teams have access to the following account information when investigating issues or responding to support requests:

**Account and workspace metadata**
- Email addresses of workspace members
- Workspace name, creation date, and plan (Free, Team, or Business)
- Billing contact name and email
- Invoice history, payment status, and subscription details
- Number of pages, projects, and tasks in the workspace

**Activity logs**
- Sign-in timestamps and IP addresses
- API request logs (timestamps, endpoints, response codes, but not request or response bodies)
- Webhook delivery attempts and failures
- Integration connection status (Slack, GitHub)

**Content metadata**
- Page titles and creation dates (but not page content)
- Task titles, keys, and statuses (but not task descriptions or comments)
- Project names and archived status
- File names and sizes (but not file contents)

**Support tickets**
- All messages exchanged with support, including attachments you send
- Diagnostic logs you export and share with support

## What staff cannot see

The following data is encrypted at rest and never accessible to Quillstack staff:

**Passwords and authentication secrets**
- Your account password (stored as a salted hash)
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) secrets
- API keys (stored encrypted; only you see the full key at creation)
- Webhook signing secrets (whsec_ prefix; stored encrypted)
- SSO certificates and private keys

**Page and task content**
- The body of any page or task description
- Comments on pages or tasks
- File attachments uploaded to pages or tasks

**Private configuration**
- Environment variables or secrets you store in pages (for example database credentials in a runbook)

Support can see page and task titles, which may contain sensitive information if you include it there. If a page title reveals confidential details, we recommend renaming it to something generic.

## Billing information access

Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged. If you contact support about an invoice or payment issue, we will verify your role in the workspace before sharing any billing information. Support never shares details about another customer's workspace, even if you claim to be affiliated with that organization.

To update the billing contact, an Owner or Admin must sign in at app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Billing > Billing contact. Once changed, billing emails and invoices are sent to the new contact.

## Data access logging

Every time a Quillstack employee accesses customer data, the action is logged in an internal audit system. Logs include the employee's identity, the workspace accessed, the reason for access (linked to a support ticket or incident), and the timestamp. These logs are reviewed monthly by the security team, and anomalous access triggers an automatic alert.

## What we never ask for

Quillstack support will never ask you for the following:

| Never requested | Why |
|-----------------|-----|
| Your password | We cannot see it and do not need it to investigate issues |
| Your 2FA code | We cannot bypass 2FA and will never ask you to disable it |
| Your API key | We can verify key metadata without seeing the key itself |
| Payment card details | All payments are processed by Stripe; we never handle card numbers |
| SSO credentials | We do not need your identity provider username or password |

If someone claiming to be from Quillstack asks for any of these, do not provide them and report the request to security@quillstack.com immediately.

## Account impersonation

In rare cases, support may ask for permission to temporarily impersonate your account to reproduce an issue. Impersonation is only used when you explicitly request it and requires approval from a senior engineer. When impersonation is active, you receive an email notification, and the session is logged in the internal audit system. Support ends the impersonation session as soon as the investigation is complete, typically within 30 minutes.
