Every invoice and receipt is available for download from your workspace billing settings. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle, numbered in the format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN, and emailed to your billing contact. You can download them as PDF files at any time, and Quillstack keeps them available for the lifetime of your workspace.

## Before you start

Only workspace Owners and Admins can access billing settings and download invoices. If you are an Editor or Viewer, you do not have permission to view the Billing section under Settings. If you need an invoice and do not have the Owner or Admin role, ask an Owner or Admin to download it for you or to change your role.

Invoices are sent by email to the billing contact on the day they are issued. If you did not receive an invoice by email, check your spam folder for messages from noreply@quillstack.com. If the invoice is not there, verify that the correct billing contact is set under Settings > Billing > Billing contact. You can update the billing contact at any time, but the change applies only to future invoices.

Receipts are the same as invoices for subscription charges. Quillstack does not issue separate receipt documents; the invoice serves as your receipt for tax and accounting purposes. If you need a document with the word "receipt" on it, download the invoice PDF and rename the file.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com as an Owner or Admin.
2. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices.
3. Find the invoice you want to download in the list. Invoices are sorted by date, with the most recent at the top.
4. Click the download icon next to the invoice number.
5. The invoice opens in a new browser tab as a PDF. Save it to your computer using your browser's save or download function.

Repeat steps 3 through 5 for each invoice you need. You can download any invoice from any billing cycle, past or present. Deleted workspaces retain their invoices for 14 days, after which they are permanently deleted along with all other workspace data.

## Invoice details

Each invoice includes the following information: invoice number in the format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN (for example INV-202608-00417), issue date, billing period, workspace name, billing contact name and address, line items with descriptions and amounts, subtotal, tax (if applicable), total amount charged, payment method (last four digits of the card or "bank transfer"), and payment status (paid or unpaid).

If you added a VAT or tax ID under Settings > Billing > Tax details, it appears on invoices issued after you saved it. Tax IDs are not applied retroactively to earlier invoices. If you need an amended invoice with your tax ID for a past billing cycle, contact your local tax authority; Quillstack does not reissue invoices.

## Bulk download

Quillstack does not currently offer a bulk download option for all invoices at once. If you need to download many invoices (for example, for an annual audit), you must download them one at a time by repeating the steps above. Most browsers let you open multiple invoices in tabs and save them all at once using a save-all-tabs extension.

## Troubleshooting

If the download link does not work or the PDF does not open, try a different browser. Some browser extensions block PDF files or interfere with downloads. Disable extensions temporarily or use an incognito or private window to download the invoice.

If an invoice is missing from the list, check that you are looking at the correct workspace. If you are a member of multiple workspaces, make sure you have selected the right one from the workspace switcher in the top left corner. Invoices are specific to each workspace and do not appear across workspaces.

If you see an invoice in the list but it shows as unpaid even though you know the payment went through, the payment may still be processing. Wait a few hours and refresh the page. If the status does not update within 24 hours, check your payment method under Settings > Billing > Payment method and contact support if the card was charged but the invoice still shows unpaid.

## Invoice format reference

| Field | Example | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Invoice number | INV-202608-00417 | Format: INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN |
| Issue date | 2024-08-01 | First day of billing cycle |
| Billing period | 2024-08-01 to 2024-08-31 | One month for monthly billing |
| Workspace name | Acme Engineering | As set in Settings > General |
| Billing contact | Jane Doe, jane@acme.com | Receives invoice emails |
