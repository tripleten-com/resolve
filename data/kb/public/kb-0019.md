If you cannot sign in to Quillstack, you can request a password reset link. The link is sent to your registered email address and lets you set a new password without needing access to your account. Reset links expire after 60 minutes and can be used only once, so you will need to request a new one if the link has expired or you have already used it.

## Before you start

You need access to the email address associated with your Quillstack account. If you no longer have access to that email, contact support from another email address and provide proof of account ownership so the support team can update your email address before you reset your password.

Quillstack staff can never see your password and support never asks for it. If someone claiming to be from Quillstack asks for your password, report it to security@quillstack.com immediately.

## Steps

1. Go to https://app.quillstack.com/sign-in and click **Forgot password?** below the sign-in form.
2. Enter the email address you use to sign in to Quillstack.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Check your email inbox for a message from Quillstack with the subject "Reset your password."
5. Click the link in the email within 60 minutes of receiving it.
6. Enter your new password twice to confirm it, then click **Reset password**.
7. Sign in with your new password.

If you do not receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. Some email providers delay or filter automated messages from new senders.

## Troubleshooting

**The reset link has expired**

Reset links expire after 60 minutes and can be used only once. If you see an error saying the link has expired or is invalid, return to the sign-in page and request a new reset link by following the steps above.

**The email never arrives**

If you do not receive the reset email after 10 minutes and it is not in your spam folder, verify that you entered the correct email address. The reset email is only sent to the address registered with your Quillstack account. If you are unsure which email address you used, try requesting a reset for each address you might have used.

**I reset my password but still cannot sign in**

After resetting your password, wait 30 seconds before attempting to sign in. If you still cannot sign in, your account may be locked after too many failed sign-in attempts. Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts, and you will see a message explaining the lockout when you try to sign in. Wait 15 minutes, then try again with your new password.

**I am being asked for a two-factor authentication code**

If you have two-factor authentication enabled, you will need to enter a code from your authenticator app after entering your new password. If you do not have access to your authenticator app, use one of your recovery codes instead. Recovery codes were provided when you enabled 2FA and each code can be used only once.

**My workspace uses SSO and I cannot reset my password**

If your workspace has enforced SSO-only sign-in with the sso.enforce setting, password sign-in is blocked for everyone except workspace Owners. If you are not an Owner and your workspace uses SSO, you must sign in through your identity provider. Password reset is not available when SSO is enforced for your role.
