Every page in Quillstack keeps a history of changes so you can see what was edited, when, and by whom. If someone deletes important content or you need to roll back to an earlier draft, you can view previous versions and restore them with one click. This article explains how version history works and how long versions are kept.

## Before you start

Version history is available on every plan, but retention varies by plan. Page version history is kept for 30 days on Free, 180 days on Team, and without limit on Business. After the retention period, older versions are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. You must be an Editor, Admin, or Owner to restore a previous version; Viewers can see version history but cannot restore.

## Steps

### View version history

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open the page whose history you want to view.
2. Click the **···** menu in the top right corner of the page and select **Version history**.
3. The version history panel opens on the right side of the screen. Each version shows the date, time, and the member who made the change.
4. Click any version in the list to preview it. The page content updates to show what the page looked like at that point in time.
5. To compare two versions, select the first version, then hold `Shift` and click a second version. Additions are highlighted in green and deletions in red.

### Restore a previous version

1. Open version history as described above and find the version you want to restore.
2. Click the version to preview it and confirm it contains the content you need.
3. Click **Restore this version** at the top of the preview pane.
4. A new version is created with the restored content, and the page returns to edit mode. The restoration itself appears as a new entry in version history, so you can undo it if needed.
5. Click **Publish** or press `Cmd+S` (Mac) or `Ctrl+S` (Windows) to save the restored version.

## Retention by plan

| Plan | Version history retention | Notes |
|------|---------------------------|-------|
| Free | 30 days | Versions older than 30 days are deleted automatically |
| Team | 180 days | Versions older than 180 days are deleted automatically |
| Business | Unlimited | All versions are kept indefinitely |

If you upgrade from Free to Team or from Team to Business, versions that were already deleted under the old retention period are not recovered. Only versions created after the upgrade are kept according to the new retention policy. For example, if you upgrade from Free to Team on day 35, versions from days 1 through 30 are gone, but versions from day 31 onward are kept for 180 days from their creation date.

## How version history works

Quillstack creates a new version every time you click **Publish** or press `Cmd+S` / `Ctrl+S`. Autosave does not create versions; only manual saves do. If multiple people edit the same page, each person's save creates a separate version, and the version history shows who made each change. Versions are stored separately from the current page content, so restoring a version does not overwrite anything permanently until you click **Publish**.

## Troubleshooting

If you do not see a version you need in the history, it may have been created more than 30 days ago on Free, more than 180 days ago on Team, or it may not have been saved manually. Check the date of the version you are looking for and compare it to your plan's retention period. If the version is older than the retention period, it has been permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

If the **Restore this version** button is grayed out, your role is Viewer. Ask an Admin or Owner to change your role to Editor under Settings > Members. If you are an Editor and the button is still grayed out, the page may be locked or archived. Check the page status in the **···** menu.

If you restore a version by mistake, open version history again and restore the version from just before the restoration. Every restore creates a new version, so you can always undo a restore by restoring the previous version.
