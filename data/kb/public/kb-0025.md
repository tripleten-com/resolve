OIDC single sign-on lets members sign in to Quillstack through your identity provider instead of entering a password. This feature is available on the Business plan and works with providers like Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace. You configure the connection once in your workspace settings, then members can sign in with one click.

## Before you start

You need the Business plan and the Owner or Admin role to configure OIDC. Your identity provider must support OpenID Connect 1.0 and provide a client ID, client secret, and discovery endpoint (also called the issuer URL or well-known configuration URL). Most providers generate these values when you register a new application. Have your provider's documentation open while you work through the setup.

SSO configuration does not affect existing sessions. Members who are already signed in stay signed in until their session expires, and new sign-ins use OIDC after you complete the setup.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Security > Single sign-on.
2. Select OIDC as the protocol.
3. Copy the redirect URI shown on the screen (it will be `https://app.quillstack.com/sso/oidc/callback`).
4. Open your identity provider's admin console and create a new OIDC application or client. Paste the redirect URI from step 3 into the allowed redirect URIs field.
5. Copy the client ID, client secret, and issuer URL (discovery endpoint) from your identity provider.
6. Return to Quillstack and paste those three values into the corresponding fields: `sso.client_id`, `sso.client_secret`, and `sso.issuer_url`.
7. Click Save settings.
8. Test the connection by opening a private browser window, going to app.quillstack.com, and clicking Sign in with SSO. Enter your workspace name and confirm that you are redirected to your identity provider and can sign in.

## Troubleshooting

If the test sign-in fails with an error from your identity provider, check that the redirect URI in your provider's application matches exactly `https://app.quillstack.com/sso/oidc/callback`. Trailing slashes, http versus https, and uppercase versus lowercase all matter.

If you see a Quillstack error page saying the issuer URL is invalid, verify that the URL you entered ends in `/.well-known/openid-configuration` or that your provider's documentation confirms it is the correct discovery endpoint. Some providers call this the metadata URL or issuer.

If members can sign in with OIDC but their name or email does not appear correctly, check the claims your provider sends in the ID token. Quillstack expects `email` and `name` claims; if your provider uses different claim names, contact support to map them.

## OIDC provider comparison

| Provider | Issuer URL format | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Okta | `https://your-domain.okta.com` | Add `/oauth2/default` if using the default authorization server |
| Azure AD | `https://login.microsoftonline.com/{tenant-id}/v2.0` | Replace `{tenant-id}` with your Azure tenant ID |
| Google Workspace | `https://accounts.google.com` | No changes needed |
| Auth0 | `https://your-domain.auth0.com/` | Include the trailing slash |
