You can add or remove seats to match your team size at any time. Quillstack adjusts your billing automatically, charging you only for the time each seat is active in the current billing cycle.

## Before you start

Only an Owner or Admin can add or remove seats. The Free plan is limited to 3 members and does not support additional seats; you must upgrade to Team or Business to expand your workspace. Log in to app.quillstack.com and navigate to Settings > Billing > Seats to manage your team size.

## Steps

**To add a seat:**

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Seats.
2. Click **Add seat**.
3. Enter the number of seats to add.
4. Review the prorated charge for the current billing cycle.
5. Click **Confirm and charge** to complete the purchase.

The charge appears on your payment method immediately. Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated: you are charged only for the days remaining in the current billing cycle. The new seats are billed at the full monthly rate starting with your next invoice.

**To remove a seat:**

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Seats.
2. Click **Remove seat**.
3. Select the members to remove or reduce the seat count.
4. Review the credit that will be applied to your next invoice.
5. Click **Confirm removal**.

Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice; removed seats are never refunded in cash. The removed members lose access to the workspace immediately.

## Pricing and proration examples

The Team plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly. The Business plan costs $28 per member per month when billed monthly. Annual billing costs 10 times the monthly rate (two months free). The table below shows prorated charges for adding one seat on Team or Business:

| Days remaining in cycle | Team prorated charge | Business prorated charge |
|-------------------------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 30 out of 30 (day 1)    | $12.00               | $28.00                   |
| 20 out of 30            | $8.00                | $18.67                   |
| 10 out of 30            | $4.00                | $9.33                    |
| 5 out of 30             | $2.00                | $4.67                    |

Removing a seat generates a credit using the same calculation. If you remove a Business seat 10 days into a 30-day cycle, the credit is $18.67 and appears on your next invoice.

## Limits

There is no maximum number of seats on Team or Business plans. The Free plan is capped at 3 members. If you remove seats and your active member count exceeds the new seat count, you must remove members from the workspace before the change is processed. Go to Settings > Members to deactivate accounts.

## Troubleshooting

**I cannot add seats because my payment method failed.** Update your payment method under Settings > Billing > Payment method, then try again. If your workspace is in read-only mode after a failed payment, updating the payment method restores full access and allows you to add seats.

**The prorated charge is higher than I expected.** Proration is based on the exact number of days in your billing cycle, which varies by month (28 to 31 days). Check the calculation on the confirmation screen before you click Confirm and charge. Your next invoice in Settings > Billing > Invoices shows the full breakdown.

**I removed a seat but the member still has access.** Seat removal is immediate, but the member's session may remain active for up to 30 minutes. Ask the member to sign out and try to sign back in. If they can still access the workspace after 30 minutes, contact support from Settings > Support.

**Can I get a refund instead of a credit when I remove a seat?** No. Removed seats always generate account credits that reduce your next invoice. Cash refunds are not issued for seat removals, even if you cancel your subscription before the credit is used.
