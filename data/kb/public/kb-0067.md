The email digest summarizes activity in your workspace and arrives once a day at 09:00 in the workspace time zone. You can choose which events to include, turn the digest on or off, and set separate preferences for each workspace you belong to. The digest is configured under Settings > Notifications > Digest.

## Before you start

You need an active Quillstack account and membership in at least one workspace. The digest settings apply only to you—other members configure their own preferences independently. Changes take effect the next morning at 09:00.

The digest respects your workspace role. You only receive updates about pages and projects you have permission to view, so Viewers see a subset of what Editors and Admins receive.

## Steps

1. Sign in at app.quillstack.com and open the workspace where you want to change digest settings.
2. Go to **Settings > Notifications > Digest**.
3. Check or uncheck the event types you want in your daily email:
   - **Pages created**: new pages added to the workspace
   - **Pages updated**: edits to existing pages
   - **Pages commented on**: new comments on pages you follow or created
   - **Tasks created**: new tasks in projects you are a member of
   - **Tasks completed**: tasks marked as done
   - **Tasks assigned to you**: tasks where you become the assignee
   - **Project milestones**: project deadlines approaching or passed
4. Set the **Delivery time zone** to match your preferred morning hour. The digest is always sent at 09:00, but you can select any time zone to control when that occurs in your local time.
5. Toggle **Enable daily digest** on or off. When disabled, you receive no digest emails from this workspace even if event types are checked.
6. Click **Save changes**.

If you belong to multiple workspaces, repeat these steps in each one. The digest is workspace-specific and cannot be configured globally across all your workspaces.

## Digest contents and format

The digest email lists activity from the previous 24 hours, grouped by event type. Each item includes a direct link to the page, task, or project. If no activity matches your selected event types, you do not receive a digest that day.

The table below shows how event types map to the information included:

| Event type                  | What you see in the digest                              |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Pages created               | Page title, creator, and timestamp                      |
| Pages updated               | Page title, last editor, and timestamp                  |
| Pages commented on          | Page title, commenter, and comment preview              |
| Tasks created               | Task key, title, project, and creator                   |
| Tasks completed             | Task key, title, project, and who completed it          |
| Tasks assigned to you       | Task key, title, project, and who assigned it           |
| Project milestones          | Project name, milestone name, and due date              |

If a single page was edited multiple times in 24 hours, the digest shows one entry with the most recent update. The same applies to tasks—repeated changes are collapsed into a single line.

## Troubleshooting

**I am not receiving the digest.** Check that **Enable daily digest** is turned on under Settings > Notifications > Digest. Verify that the email address on your account (Settings > Account > Email) is correct and that digest emails are not going to your spam folder. If no activity matched your selected event types in the past 24 hours, no digest is sent.

**The digest arrives at the wrong time.** The delivery time zone is set separately from your account time zone. Go to Settings > Notifications > Digest and confirm the **Delivery time zone** matches your location. The digest is always sent at 09:00 in that time zone.

**I see activity from projects I do not belong to.** You may have the **Pages updated** or **Pages created** event types enabled. These show all pages you can view, even if they are not linked to a project you are a member of. To narrow the digest, uncheck those event types and rely on task-specific events instead.

**The digest does not include attachments or full page content.** The digest is a summary with links. Click any item to open the full page or task in your browser.
