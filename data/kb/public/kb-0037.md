You can update your payment method at any time from your workspace billing settings. When you save a new card, Quillstack uses it for all future charges. If your workspace is in read-only mode after a failed payment, updating your payment method restores full access immediately.

## Before you start

You must be the Owner or an Admin of the workspace to update payment methods. Viewers and Editors do not have access to billing settings.

Quillstack accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Bank transfer with net-30 terms is available only on Business annual subscriptions. If you want to switch to bank transfer, contact support after upgrading to Business annual billing.

Updating your payment method does not trigger a new charge. Your next invoice is issued on the first day of your billing cycle, as usual. If a payment has already failed and is being retried, the new card is charged immediately to bring your account current.

## Steps

1. Sign in at app.quillstack.com and go to **Settings > Billing > Payment method**.
2. Click **Update payment method**.
3. Enter your new card number, expiry date, CVV, and billing postal code.
4. Click **Save**.

The new card is validated and saved within a few seconds. You see a confirmation message when the update succeeds. If the card is declined during validation, you see an error and the old payment method remains on file.

If your workspace is in read-only mode because of a failed payment, saving a valid card restores full access immediately. You do not need to wait for the next billing cycle.

## When the new card is charged

The timing depends on your workspace status:

| Workspace status | When the new card is charged |
|------------------|------------------------------|
| Active subscription | On the first day of the next billing cycle |
| Failed payment, retries pending | Immediately after you save the card |
| Read-only mode after payment failure | Immediately after you save the card |

If your workspace is active and no payment has failed, the new card is not charged until your next invoice date. You can see that date under **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.

If a payment has failed and automatic retries are still running, Quillstack charges the new card immediately to clear the outstanding balance. The charge amount matches the failed invoice.

## Troubleshooting

**The new card was declined during validation**

Check that the card number, expiry, and CVV are correct. If the card is valid and the decline persists, contact your bank. Some banks block the initial validation charge (usually $1 or less) because it comes from a new merchant. Your bank can whitelist Quillstack and allow the validation to succeed.

**I updated the card but my workspace is still in read-only mode**

Read-only mode is lifted within 60 seconds after a successful charge. If more than 5 minutes have passed, sign out and sign back in. If the issue persists, check **Settings > Billing > Invoices** to confirm the latest invoice is marked paid. Contact support if the invoice still shows as unpaid after you updated the card.

**I need to switch to bank transfer**

Bank transfer with net-30 terms is available only on Business annual subscriptions. If you are on a monthly plan or the Team plan, first upgrade to Business annual under **Settings > Billing > Change plan**. Then contact support to request bank transfer. The billing team replies within 3 business days with wire instructions and updates your workspace settings.

**Can I save multiple cards?**

No. Quillstack stores one payment method per workspace. When you save a new card, it replaces the old one. If you need to alternate between two cards, update the payment method before each billing cycle.

**The card I want to remove is still being charged**

If you updated your payment method but an old card was charged, check the invoice date. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle. If you updated the card after the invoice was issued, the old card was charged for that cycle and the new card will be used next time. If the charge happened after you saved the new card, contact support with the invoice number (format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN) and the last four digits of both cards.
