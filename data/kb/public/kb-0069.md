The Quillstack mobile app lets you work on pages and tasks from your phone or tablet. This guide walks through common mobile app issues and how to fix them, covering sign-in problems, sync errors, and feature availability.

## Before you start

The mobile app requires iOS 16 or later or Android 11 or later. Check your device's operating system version in Settings if the app fails to install. Mobile app sessions expire after 90 days of inactivity, which is longer than web sessions (30 days). Billing settings cannot be changed from the mobile app—you must sign in at app.quillstack.com to manage subscriptions, payment methods, or invoices.

## Steps

### Fix sign-in issues

1. Open the Quillstack mobile app and tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. If you see "Invalid credentials," reset your password at app.quillstack.com/reset and try again.
4. If your account uses two-factor authentication, enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app when prompted.
5. If you see "Account locked," wait 15 minutes—accounts lock automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
6. If your workspace requires SSO (SAML or OIDC), tap **Sign in with SSO** and enter your workspace domain instead of using a password.

### Resolve sync problems

1. Check that your device has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data).
2. Pull down on any page or task list to force a manual sync.
3. If offline edits do not appear after reconnecting, force-quit the app and reopen it—sync runs automatically on launch.
4. Conflicts are resolved as last write wins: if you edit the same page offline on two devices, the most recent save overwrites earlier changes.
5. If a page or task remains out of sync after 5 minutes, sign out and sign back in to clear the local cache.

## Troubleshooting

**The app crashes on launch.** Delete and reinstall the app from the App Store or Google Play Store. Your data is stored in the cloud and will sync after you sign back in.

**I cannot find Settings > Billing in the mobile app.** Billing settings are only available at app.quillstack.com. Sign in on a desktop or mobile browser to update payment methods, view invoices, or change your plan.

**Attachments fail to download.** Large files may time out on slow connections. Switch to Wi-Fi and tap the attachment again. The maximum file size is 100 MB on every plan.

**Push notifications do not arrive.** Check that notifications are enabled for Quillstack in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Notifications > Quillstack; Android: Settings > Apps > Quillstack > Notifications). Notification preferences are managed under Settings > Notifications at app.quillstack.com.

**The app shows "Session expired."** Mobile sessions last 90 days. Tap **Sign in again** and enter your credentials. If you see this message every few hours, check that your device's date and time are set to automatic.

## Feature availability

The table below shows which features are available in the mobile app versus the web app:

| Feature | Mobile app | Web app |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Create and edit pages | Yes | Yes |
| Create and update tasks | Yes | Yes |
| View projects (board, list, timeline) | Yes | Yes |
| Offline editing | Yes | No |
| Billing and subscription settings | No | Yes |
| API key management | No | Yes |
| Workspace exports | No | Yes |
| Audit log (Business) | No | Yes |
