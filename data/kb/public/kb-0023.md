If you enter the wrong password too many times when signing in to Quillstack, your account is locked temporarily to protect it from unauthorized access. The lock expires automatically, and you can also reset your password to regain access immediately. This article explains how the lockout works and what to do if you are locked out.

## Before you start

Account lockouts apply to individual user accounts, not to entire workspaces. If one member is locked out, other members can still sign in normally. The lockout is triggered by failed attempts on the web app at app.quillstack.com and on the mobile app, but successful sign-ins from either surface reset the failure counter.

You do not need to contact support to unlock your account. The lock expires on its own, and resetting your password unlocks it immediately.

## How the lockout works

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. Failed attempts are counted over a rolling 30-minute window, so if you enter the wrong password twice, wait 20 minutes, and then fail three more times, the account locks after the fifth failure.

The 15-minute lockout begins from the moment of the fifth failed attempt. During this time, every sign-in attempt returns an error message stating that the account is temporarily locked, even if you enter the correct password. The timer does not reset if you try again during the lockout period.

Once the 15 minutes have passed, the failure counter resets to zero and you can sign in normally. If you fail five more times after the lock expires, the account locks again for another 15 minutes.

| Event | Failure count | Account status |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| First wrong password | 1 | Unlocked |
| Second wrong password | 2 | Unlocked |
| Third wrong password | 3 | Unlocked |
| Fourth wrong password | 4 | Unlocked |
| Fifth wrong password | 5 | Locked for 15 minutes |
| Wait 15 minutes | 0 | Unlocked, counter reset |

## Steps

If your account is locked, you have two options: wait for the lockout to expire, or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

### Option 1: Wait for the lockout to expire

1. Note the time when you received the lockout message.
2. Wait 15 minutes from that moment.
3. Return to the sign-in page and enter your correct password.

The account unlocks automatically after 15 minutes, and you do not need to take any other action.

### Option 2: Reset your password

1. Go to the sign-in page at app.quillstack.com.
2. Click **Forgot password?** below the password field.
3. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**.
4. Check your email for a message from Quillstack with the subject "Reset your password."
5. Click the link in the email (the link is valid for 60 minutes and can be used only once).
6. Enter a new password and click **Reset password**.

Resetting your password unlocks the account immediately, even if the 15-minute lockout period has not yet passed. The password reset link expires after 60 minutes, so if you do not use it in time you must request a new one.

After you reset your password, the failure counter resets to zero and you can sign in with the new password right away.

## Troubleshooting

**I did not receive the password reset email.** Check your spam or junk folder. If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, request a new reset link. Make sure you are entering the email address that is registered to your Quillstack account.

**The password reset link says it has expired.** Password reset links expire after 60 minutes and can be used only once. If the link has expired or you already used it, return to the sign-in page and request a new reset link.

**I reset my password but still cannot sign in.** If you reset your password and then fail five more sign-in attempts, the account locks again for another 15 minutes. Wait for the lockout to expire, or contact your workspace Owner or Admin to confirm that your account has not been removed from the workspace.

**My account locks repeatedly.** If you are locked out multiple times in a short period, you may be entering the wrong password or using a saved password that is out of date. Reset your password and update any saved credentials in your browser or password manager. If lockouts continue after you are certain you are using the correct password, contact support at the address under Settings > Help.
