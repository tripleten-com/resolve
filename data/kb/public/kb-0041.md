You can cancel your Quillstack subscription at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, so you keep full access until then. After cancellation, your workspace is not deleted automatically, but you must act within 14 days if you want to preserve your data.

## Before you start

Only the workspace Owner can cancel a subscription. If you are an Admin, Editor, or Viewer, ask the Owner to cancel. Plan upgrades take effect immediately; plan downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open the workspace you want to cancel.
2. Navigate to Settings > Billing > Subscription.
3. Click **Cancel subscription**.
4. Review the cancellation date shown (the last day of your current billing cycle).
5. Select a reason for canceling from the dropdown (optional).
6. Click **Confirm cancellation**.

You receive a confirmation email immediately. Your workspace remains fully functional until the end of the current billing cycle. After that date, the workspace switches to the Free plan if it meets Free plan limits, or becomes read-only if it exceeds those limits.

## What happens after cancellation

After the cancellation takes effect, your workspace is not deleted. If your workspace has 3 or fewer members and 1 workspace, it switches to the Free plan automatically. If it exceeds Free plan limits, it becomes read-only. You can still view your data, export it, or delete the workspace.

A deleted workspace is retained for 14 days and can be restored by an Owner from Settings > Workspaces > Deleted; after 14 days it is permanently deleted. If you want to keep your data, export the workspace before deleting it.

## Refunds

Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge. If you cancel within 14 days of being charged, you can request a refund by contacting support. Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team, which replies within 3 business days. Refunds are never issued automatically.

If you cancel after 14 days, no refund is available. You keep access until the end of the billing cycle you already paid for.

## Troubleshooting

**I canceled but was charged again.** Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. If you canceled mid-cycle, you are charged for the remainder of that cycle, but no further charges occur after the cancellation date.

**I want to cancel immediately and get a refund.** Cancellations always take effect at the end of the billing cycle. If you were charged within the last 14 days, contact support to request a refund. The billing team reviews every refund request and replies within 3 business days.

**I canceled by mistake. How do I reactivate my subscription?** If the cancellation has not yet taken effect, go to Settings > Billing > Subscription and click **Reactivate subscription**. If the cancellation already took effect and your workspace is on the Free plan or read-only, upgrade to a paid plan from Settings > Billing > Plans.
