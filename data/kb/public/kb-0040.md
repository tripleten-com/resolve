Quillstack supports three billing currencies: USD, EUR, and GBP. You choose the currency when you create your workspace, and it determines the currency for all future invoices and charges. The currency setting cannot be changed after workspace creation, so if you need to switch currencies you must create a new workspace and migrate your content.

## Before you start

Your billing currency affects every charge to your workspace, including subscription fees, additional seat charges, and any overage fees. The currency does not change the features available on your plan or the relative pricing between Free, Team, and Business tiers. If you have not yet created your workspace, decide which currency you prefer before starting the setup process.

Only the workspace Owner can view or manage billing settings, including the currency displayed on invoices. If you are not the Owner, ask them to check Settings > Billing > Overview to confirm which currency your workspace uses.

## Steps

### Check your workspace's billing currency

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open the workspace you want to check.
2. Navigate to Settings > Billing > Overview.
3. Look for the Currency field in the billing details section.
4. The three-letter code (USD, EUR, or GBP) shows the currency for all invoices.

Your most recent invoice also displays the currency. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices and open any invoice to see the currency symbol and code at the top of the document.

### Choose a currency when creating a workspace

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and click Create workspace in the sidebar.
2. Enter a workspace name and choose your plan (Free, Team, or Business).
3. On the billing setup screen, select your preferred currency from the dropdown: USD, EUR, or GBP.
4. Complete the remaining setup steps, including payment method if you chose Team or Business.
5. Click Finish setup to create the workspace.

The currency you select here applies to every invoice issued to this workspace. Once the workspace is created, this setting is permanent and cannot be edited.

## How currency affects pricing

The table below shows monthly per-member pricing for paid plans in each supported currency:

| Plan     | USD    | EUR    | GBP    |
|----------|--------|--------|--------|
| Team     | $12    | €12    | £10    |
| Business | $28    | €28    | £24    |

Annual billing costs ten times the monthly price in the same currency, which gives you two months free compared to paying monthly. For example, Team annual in USD is $120 per member per year, while Team annual in EUR is €120 per member per year.

Exchange rates do not affect your pricing. If you are billed in EUR, you always pay the EUR price regardless of exchange rate fluctuations between EUR and other currencies. Your bank or payment processor may apply conversion fees if your card is denominated in a different currency, but Quillstack does not control those fees.

## Troubleshooting

**I need to change my workspace currency but the setting is locked.** The currency cannot be changed after workspace creation. If you must use a different currency, export your workspace content from Settings > Data > Export, create a new workspace with the correct currency, and import the exported data. Keep in mind that workspace exports can take up to 24 hours and the download link is valid for 7 days. Your old workspace can then be deleted if you no longer need it.

**My card is in a different currency than my workspace.** You can pay with a card denominated in any currency, but your card issuer or payment processor will convert the charge from your workspace's billing currency to your card's currency. Quillstack does not control the exchange rate or conversion fees applied by your bank. Check with your card issuer if you have questions about international transaction fees.

**Can I switch between monthly and annual billing without changing currency?** Yes. Changing from monthly to annual billing or vice versa does not affect your currency. Go to Settings > Billing > Plan and click Change billing cycle. Your currency remains the same and only the billing frequency changes.

**I see a different currency on my invoice than I expected.** Invoices are always issued in the workspace's billing currency, which was set when the workspace was created. If you believe the currency is incorrect, verify it under Settings > Billing > Overview. If you need a copy of your invoice in a different currency for accounting purposes, you must handle the conversion yourself; Quillstack does not issue invoices in multiple currencies for the same workspace.
