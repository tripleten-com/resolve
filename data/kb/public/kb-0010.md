Public page links let you share a Quillstack page with anyone, even people who are not members of your workspace. Visitors with the link can view the page but cannot edit it or see other pages in your workspace. Public links are available on the Team and Business plans only.

## Before you start

The Free plan does not support public page links. On Free, you can only share pages with members of your workspace. If you need to share a page publicly, upgrade to Team or Business.

Anyone with a public link can view the page, so do not use public links for sensitive or confidential content. If you need to control who sees a page, invite those people to your workspace as members instead of sharing a public link.

## Steps

1. Open the page you want to share and click **Share** in the top-right corner.
2. Under **Anyone with the link**, toggle the switch to **On**.
3. Copy the link that appears and send it to anyone you want to share the page with.
4. To stop sharing the page publicly, return to the Share menu and toggle **Anyone with the link** to **Off**.

When you disable a public link, anyone who tries to visit it sees an error message and cannot view the page. Existing workspace members are not affected and can still access the page normally.

## Public link permissions

| Action                        | Public link visitor | Workspace member |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| View the page                 | Yes                 | Yes              |
| Edit the page                 | No                  | Yes (Editor+)    |
| See other workspace pages     | No                  | Yes              |
| See who edited the page       | No                  | Yes              |
| Download attachments          | Yes                 | Yes              |

Public link visitors can download files attached to the page, so be careful when sharing pages that contain sensitive documents or images.

## Troubleshooting

**I do not see "Anyone with the link" in the Share menu.** Public links are only available on Team and Business. If your workspace is on the Free plan, upgrade to Team or Business to enable this feature.

**The public link shows an error when I visit it.** The page owner may have disabled the public link, deleted the page, or moved it to a workspace that does not support public links. Ask the page owner to check whether the link is still enabled.

**Can I password-protect a public link?** No. Public links are either on or off. If you need to restrict access, invite specific people to your workspace as Viewers instead of using a public link.

**Can I see who visited a public link?** No. Quillstack does not track or log public link visits. If you need to know who viewed a page, share it with workspace members instead so their activity appears in the page history.
