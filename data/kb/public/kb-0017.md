Transferring ownership reassigns the Owner role to another member of your workspace and demotes you to Admin. This is a permanent action that cannot be undone, so only the current Owner can initiate the transfer and only after confirming they understand the consequences. You might transfer ownership when you leave the team, when someone else takes over billing responsibility, or when you want to hand off workspace management.

## Before you start

Only the current Owner can transfer ownership. The person receiving ownership must already be a member of the workspace with any role—Viewer, Editor, or Admin. You cannot transfer ownership to someone outside the workspace, so invite them first if needed. Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer; only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact.

## What happens during the transfer

When you transfer ownership, three things change immediately:

1. The new Owner gains all Owner permissions, including the ability to delete the workspace, transfer ownership again, and change the billing contact.
2. You are automatically demoted to Admin, so you keep most permissions but lose the three Owner-only actions.
3. The billing contact does not change. If you want the new Owner to receive invoices, they must update the billing contact separately after the transfer.

The transfer takes effect the moment you confirm it. There is no waiting period and no way to reverse it unless the new Owner transfers ownership back to you.

## Steps

### Transfer ownership to another member

1. Go to Settings > Members.
2. Find the person you want to make the new Owner.
3. Click the three-dot menu next to their name and select Transfer ownership.
4. Read the warning dialog carefully.
5. Type the workspace name to confirm.
6. Click Transfer ownership.

You are now an Admin and the other person is the Owner. They receive an email notification about the change and can immediately perform Owner-only actions.

### What the new Owner should do next

After receiving ownership, the new Owner should:

- Verify they can access Settings > Billing and see the current plan and payment method.
- Decide whether to change the billing contact to their own email address (Settings > Billing > Billing contact).
- Review the member list and roles to ensure everyone has appropriate permissions.
- Check security settings like two-factor authentication requirements and SSO configuration.

## Troubleshooting

**I transferred ownership by mistake.** Ask the new Owner to transfer ownership back to you. If they refuse or are unavailable, contact support, but support cannot override the new Owner's decision. The transfer is final and support will only assist the current Owner.

**The Transfer ownership option is grayed out.** You can only transfer ownership to an existing member. If the person you want is not in the workspace yet, go to Settings > Members > Invite member and add them first.

**I transferred ownership but I still receive invoices.** The billing contact is a separate setting and does not change automatically. The new Owner must go to Settings > Billing > Billing contact and update it to their email address. Invoices are numbered INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN and issued on the first day of each billing cycle.

**Can I transfer ownership to someone on a different email domain?** Yes. Ownership can be transferred to any member regardless of their email domain, but if you use SSO enforcement the new Owner must be able to sign in through your identity provider.

**I am the Owner and I am leaving the company. What should I do?** Transfer ownership to another team member before you leave. If you delete your account without transferring ownership, the workspace may become inaccessible and support will need to intervene, which can take several days.
