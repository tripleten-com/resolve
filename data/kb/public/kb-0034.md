If you see two identical charges from Quillstack on the same day, one is likely a duplicate. Quillstack's billing system detects and reverses duplicate charges automatically, but you can also request manual review if you believe you were charged incorrectly.

## Before you start

Check your bank or card statement to confirm that both charges posted (not just pending authorizations, which may appear twice but only settle once). Duplicate charges are always for the exact same amount on the same day. If the amounts differ or the dates are different, the charges are not duplicates; they may be legitimate charges for separate billing events like adding a seat or upgrading mid-cycle.

## Steps

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices and find the invoice numbers for both charges.
2. Compare the invoice dates, amounts, and line items.
3. If the invoices are identical and both were issued within 24 hours, contact support from Settings > Support.
4. Include both invoice numbers (formatted INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN) and a screenshot of your bank statement showing both charges.
5. The billing team reviews the request and replies within 3 business days.

If the same card is charged the same amount twice within 24 hours, the second charge is a duplicate; after review, the billing team reverses it within 5 business days. Refunds are never issued automatically or by the support assistant; every refund request is reviewed by the billing team, which replies within 3 business days. Once the reversal is processed, the charge disappears from your statement or appears as a credit, depending on your bank's policies.

## What causes duplicate charges

Duplicate charges are rare and usually result from a temporary issue in the payment gateway or a double-click during checkout. Quillstack's billing system includes safeguards to prevent duplicates, but transient network errors can occasionally bypass them. The system flags suspected duplicates for automatic review, but manual review is faster if you contact support directly.

## Common non-duplicates

The following are not duplicate charges, even though they may appear similar:

| Scenario                                      | Why it is not a duplicate                                                                 |
|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Two charges a few days apart                  | These are separate billing events, such as your monthly renewal and a mid-cycle seat addition. |
| Same amount but different invoice numbers     | Each invoice represents a distinct charge; check the line items to see what each covers.  |
| One charge posted, one pending                | Pending authorizations sometimes appear before the final charge and then drop off.        |
| Two charges in different months               | These are your regular monthly invoices; Quillstack bills on the same day each cycle.     |

If you are unsure whether a charge is a duplicate, include both invoice numbers when you contact support. The billing team can explain each charge and confirm whether a reversal is warranted.

## Troubleshooting

**I was charged twice but only have one invoice.** Check Settings > Billing > Invoices again; invoices can take up to 1 hour to appear after a charge. If only one invoice exists after 1 hour, the second charge may be a pending authorization that will drop off within 3 business days. Contact your bank to confirm the status of both charges.

**The billing team said the charges are not duplicates, but they look identical to me.** The billing team checks the transaction IDs in the payment gateway, not just the amounts. If the transaction IDs differ, the charges are separate events even if the amounts match. Ask the billing team to explain what each charge covers; they can provide a breakdown of the line items.

**I was charged twice last month and the reversal has not appeared.** Reversals can take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank's processing time. If more than 10 business days have passed since the billing team confirmed the reversal, contact your bank with the transaction ID from the reversal confirmation email.

**Can I get the reversal as account credit instead of a refund to my card?** No. Duplicate charge reversals always go back to the original payment method. Account credits are only used for removed seats and plan downgrades, not for erroneous charges.
