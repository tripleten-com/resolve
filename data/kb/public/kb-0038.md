Quillstack offers two billing cycles: monthly and annual. Monthly billing charges your payment method once per month, while annual billing charges the full year up front and gives you two months free. You can switch between cycles at any time, and the change takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

## Before you start

You must be the workspace Owner or an Admin to change the billing cycle. The current cycle and next billing date are shown under Settings > Billing > Subscription. Annual billing is available on both the Team and Business plans, but not on Free.

## How pricing works

Monthly billing charges you each month based on the number of seats in your workspace. The Team plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly, and the Business plan costs $28 per member per month when billed monthly. If you add or remove seats mid-cycle, proration applies: new seats are charged for the remaining days in the current cycle, and removed seats create a credit toward your next invoice.

Annual billing charges the full year up front at a rate of 10 times the monthly price, which gives you two months free compared to paying monthly for twelve months. For example, a Team workspace with 5 members costs $60 per month on monthly billing or $600 per year on annual billing (equivalent to $50 per month). A Business workspace with 10 members costs $280 per month or $2,800 per year. The annual charge is issued immediately when you switch to annual billing or when you upgrade from Free to a paid plan with annual billing selected.

| Plan | Monthly (per member) | Annual (per member) | Annual savings |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Team | $12/month | $120/year | 2 months free |
| Business | $28/month | $280/year | 2 months free |

## Payment methods

Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. You can update your card at any time under Settings > Billing > Payment method. Bank transfer with net-30 terms is available only on Business annual subscriptions. To request bank transfer, contact support after upgrading to Business annual billing, and the billing team will send payment instructions and adjust your invoice terms within 3 business days.

## Steps

### Switch from monthly to annual billing

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Billing > Subscription.
2. Click **Change billing cycle**.
3. Select **Annual** and review the charge amount, which reflects 10 times your current monthly cost.
4. Click **Confirm and switch to annual**.
5. You are charged immediately for the next 12 months, and your next billing date moves forward by one year.

### Switch from annual to monthly billing

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Billing > Subscription.
2. Click **Change billing cycle**.
3. Select **Monthly** and review the confirmation message.
4. Click **Confirm and switch to monthly**.
5. The change takes effect at the end of your current annual period, and you are not refunded for unused months. Your next invoice after the annual period ends will be charged monthly.

## When changes take effect

Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately: you are charged the annual amount right away, and any unused time from your monthly cycle is credited toward the annual charge. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual period. You continue to have access for the full year you paid for, and monthly billing begins after that year ends. No refund is issued for the remaining months when you switch from annual to monthly.

## Troubleshooting

**The annual charge failed and my workspace is in read-only mode.** Update your payment method under Settings > Billing > Payment method. The charge is retried automatically, and your workspace is restored to full access as soon as the payment succeeds. If you need to switch back to monthly billing, contact support.

**I switched to annual but want a refund.** Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge. Contact support with your workspace name and invoice number (format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN), and the billing team will review your request and reply within 3 business days.

**Can I pay annually by invoice instead of card?** Yes, but only on Business annual subscriptions. Upgrade to Business annual first, then contact support to request bank transfer with net-30 terms.
