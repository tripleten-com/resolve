Quillstack sends notifications when someone mentions you, assigns you a task, or comments on a page you follow. You control which events trigger an email and which appear only in the in-app notification center. Notification preferences live under Settings > Notifications and apply to the current workspace only.

## Before you start

Changes to notification settings take effect immediately for new events. You cannot retroactively suppress or resend notifications for events that already happened. If you belong to multiple workspaces, configure notifications separately in each one. The notification center icon in the top right of app.quillstack.com shows a badge when you have unread notifications, and clicking it opens a list of recent activity.

## Steps

**To configure email notifications:**

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open Settings > Notifications > Email.
2. Toggle each event type on or off. Available events include mentions, task assignments, comments on pages you created or follow, project updates, and workspace invitations.
3. Click **Save preferences** at the bottom of the page.

**To configure in-app notifications:**

1. Go to Settings > Notifications > In-app.
2. Select which events should appear in the notification center. Most users keep all in-app events enabled and use email toggles to reduce inbox volume.
3. Click **Save preferences**.

**To mark notifications as read:**

1. Click the notification center icon in the top right.
2. Click **Mark all as read** to clear the badge, or click individual notifications to mark them read one at a time.
3. Notifications older than 30 days are removed automatically.

## Notification event types

The table below lists every event type and where it appears by default:

| Event type | Email (default) | In-app (default) | Description |
|------------|----------------|------------------|-------------|
| Mention | On | On | Someone types @yourname in a page or comment |
| Task assigned | On | On | A task is assigned to you or reassigned from you |
| Comment | Off | On | A new comment on a page you created or follow |
| Page shared | On | On | Someone shares a page with you directly |
| Project archived | Off | On | A project you are a member of is archived |
| Workspace invite | On | On | You are invited to join a new workspace |

You can override any of these defaults. For example, if you prefer to check the notification center manually, turn off email for comments and page shares while keeping task assignments on.

## Troubleshooting

**I am not receiving any email notifications.** Check your spam folder first. If Quillstack emails are landing in spam, add noreply@quillstack.com to your contacts. Then verify that your email address is correct under Settings > Account > Email and that you have at least one event type enabled under Settings > Notifications > Email.

**I receive emails for events I disabled.** Notification settings apply only to the workspace where you changed them. If you belong to multiple workspaces, open each one and configure notifications separately. Also verify that you clicked **Save preferences** after making changes; unsaved edits are discarded when you navigate away.

**The notification center badge shows a count but the list is empty.** Notifications older than 30 days are removed but may still contribute to the unread count. Click **Mark all as read** to reset the badge. If the badge persists, sign out and sign back in.

**I want notifications for every change on a specific page.** Open the page, click the three-dot menu in the top right, and select **Follow**. You will receive notifications for all comments and edits on that page, subject to your email and in-app preferences. To stop following, return to the same menu and select **Unfollow**.

**Can I set different notification preferences for different projects?** No. Notification settings apply workspace-wide and cannot be customized per project or per page. If you need granular control, use the follow and unfollow feature on individual pages.
