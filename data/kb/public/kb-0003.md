Inviting team members to your workspace lets them create pages, track tasks, and collaborate with the rest of your team. Each member has a role that controls what they can do, from viewing pages to managing billing. This guide explains how to invite members, assign roles, and manage permissions.

## Before you start

You must be an Admin or Owner to invite members to a workspace. Editors and Viewers cannot send invitations. The Free plan is limited to 3 members and 1 workspace, so if you already have 3 members you must upgrade to Team or Business before inviting more people. Check your current member count under Settings > Members. Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer; only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact.

## Steps

### Invite a new member

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open the workspace where you want to invite someone.
2. Go to Settings > Members and click **Invite member**.
3. Enter the person's email address in the field provided.
4. Choose a role from the dropdown: Viewer, Editor, Admin, or Owner. Most team members should be Editors, who can create and edit pages and tasks but cannot manage billing or workspace settings.
5. Click **Send invitation**. The person receives an email with a link to accept the invitation and create an account or sign in.
6. The invitation expires after 7 days. If it is not accepted, you can resend it from Settings > Members > Pending invitations.

### Change a member's role

1. Go to Settings > Members and find the person whose role you want to change.
2. Click the role dropdown next to their name and select the new role.
3. Click **Save**. The change takes effect immediately and the member's permissions update the next time they load a page or refresh their browser.

## Roles and permissions

| Role | Can view pages | Can edit pages | Can manage members | Can manage billing | Can delete workspace |
|------|----------------|----------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------------------|
| Viewer | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Admin | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Editors can create and edit pages, upload files, create projects, and manage tasks. Admins can do everything Editors can, plus invite and remove members, change roles, and update billing details. Owners have full control, including the ability to delete the workspace and transfer ownership to another member. Each workspace has exactly one Owner, but you can have multiple Admins.

## Troubleshooting

If you see "Member limit reached" when trying to invite someone, you are on the Free plan and already have 3 members. Upgrade to Team or Business from Settings > Billing to add more people. The upgrade takes effect immediately and you can send the invitation right away.

If an invitation email does not arrive, ask the recipient to check their spam folder. Invitations are sent from noreply@quillstack.com. If the email is still missing after 5 minutes, resend the invitation from Settings > Members > Pending invitations. If the invitation expired, delete it and send a new one.

If you cannot change a member's role, check that you are an Admin or Owner. Only Admins and Owners can change roles. If you are trying to change the Owner role, you must be the current Owner and use the Transfer ownership flow under Settings > Members, not the role dropdown.
