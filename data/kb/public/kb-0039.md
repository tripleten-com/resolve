If your business is registered for VAT or another tax, you can add your tax identification number to your Quillstack invoices. The tax ID appears on every invoice issued after you save it, but it is not applied retroactively to earlier invoices. This article explains how to add or update your tax details and what to do if you need a corrected invoice for a billing cycle that has already passed.

## Before you start

Only workspace Owners and Admins can add or change tax details. If you do not see Settings > Billing in the navigation, ask an Owner or Admin to add the tax ID for you or to change your role.

Tax details are saved at the workspace level. If you manage multiple workspaces, you must add the tax ID separately in each one. The setting applies to all future invoices for that workspace, regardless of which plan you are on.

Quillstack does not validate the format of the tax ID you enter. You are responsible for ensuring the number is correct and matches the format required by your tax authority. Common formats include VAT numbers (for example GB123456789), EU VAT IDs, Australian ABNs, Canadian GST numbers, and similar identifiers.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Quillstack at app.quillstack.com and open the workspace where you want to add a tax ID.
2. Go to **Settings > Billing > Tax details**.
3. In the **Tax ID** field, enter your VAT number or other tax registration identifier exactly as it appears on your official registration documents.
4. Optionally, add your full legal business name in the **Legal name** field if it differs from your workspace name. This name appears on invoices alongside the tax ID.
5. Click **Save**.

The tax ID is now saved. Invoices issued on or after the first day of the next billing cycle will include the tax ID in the billing details section. You can verify this by checking your next invoice under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## When the tax ID appears on invoices

Invoices are numbered INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN (for example INV-202608-00417), issued on the first day of each billing cycle, emailed to the billing contact, and available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. The tax ID you add appears on invoices issued after it is saved and is not applied to earlier invoices.

If you add a tax ID on June 10 and your billing cycle starts on the 1st of each month, the June invoice (issued June 1) will not include the tax ID, but the July invoice (issued July 1) will. This is true even if you add the tax ID partway through a billing cycle.

| When you add the tax ID | Invoice issued | Includes tax ID? |
|--------------------------|----------------|------------------|
| May 25                   | June 1         | Yes              |
| June 10                  | June 1         | No               |
| June 10                  | July 1         | Yes              |

## Updating or removing a tax ID

To change your tax ID, return to Settings > Billing > Tax details, update the field, and click Save. The new tax ID appears on invoices issued after the change. To remove a tax ID entirely, clear the field and save.

Changes to the tax ID follow the same rule as adding one: they apply only to invoices issued after the change is saved. If you correct a typo in your tax ID, earlier invoices will still show the incorrect version.

## What to do if you need a corrected invoice

Because tax details are not applied retroactively, you cannot regenerate an old invoice with a new tax ID through the web interface. If you need a corrected invoice for a billing cycle that has already been invoiced—for example, because you added your VAT number after the invoice was issued—contact support with the invoice number and the tax ID you want added. The billing team will issue a replacement invoice within 3 business days.

Include the full invoice number (for example INV-202608-00417) and the exact tax ID in your request. The replacement invoice will have the same invoice number and date as the original but will include the tax details in the billing section.

## Troubleshooting

**The tax ID does not appear on my latest invoice.** Check the issue date of the invoice. If the invoice was issued before you saved the tax ID, it will not include it. Wait for the next billing cycle and verify the new invoice includes the tax details. If the next invoice still does not show the tax ID, confirm you saved it under Settings > Billing > Tax details in the correct workspace.

**I entered the wrong tax ID and already received an invoice with it.** Update the tax ID under Settings > Billing > Tax details and save the change. Future invoices will use the corrected number. If you need a replacement invoice for the billing cycle that used the incorrect tax ID, contact support with the invoice number and the correct tax ID.

**I manage multiple workspaces and the tax ID only appears on one.** Tax details are workspace-specific. Add the tax ID separately under Settings > Billing > Tax details in each workspace where you need it to appear on invoices.
