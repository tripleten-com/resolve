You can change the email address on your Quillstack account at any time from your account settings. The new address becomes your username for signing in and receives all notifications and password reset links. Changing your email does not affect your workspace memberships, role, or billing details.

## Before you start

You must verify the new email address before the change takes effect. Quillstack sends a confirmation link to the new address, and you have 60 minutes to click it. If you do not verify within that window, the change is canceled and your old email address remains active.

The new email address cannot already be registered to another Quillstack account. If you see an error saying the address is already in use, you will need to delete or change the email on the other account first.

Changing your email does not change the billing contact for workspaces you own. Invoices continue to go to the billing contact set under Settings > Billing > Billing contact. If you want invoices sent to your new address, update the billing contact separately after you complete the email change.

## Steps

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and go to Settings > Account > Profile.
2. Click Edit next to your current email address.
3. Enter the new email address and your current password, then click Save.
4. Quillstack sends a confirmation email to the new address with the subject "Confirm your new email address".
5. Open that email and click the confirmation link within 60 minutes.
6. You are redirected to Quillstack and see a success message. Your email address is now updated.

The next time you sign in, use the new email address as your username. Your password stays the same.

## Troubleshooting

If you do not receive the confirmation email within a few minutes, check your spam folder. The email comes from noreply@quillstack.com and may be filtered by aggressive spam rules. Add that address to your contacts or safe sender list, then request a new confirmation link by repeating the steps above.

If the confirmation link says it has expired, you took longer than 60 minutes to click it. Go back to Settings > Account > Profile and start the process again. The 60-minute timer begins when you click Save in step 3, not when you open the email.

If you entered the wrong new email address by mistake and cannot access it to confirm, wait 60 minutes for the change to expire. Your old email address will remain active, and you can try again with the correct address. Quillstack support cannot manually change your email address or bypass the confirmation step.

If you forget your password after changing your email, go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password". Enter your new email address (the one you just confirmed). You will receive a password reset link that expires after 60 minutes and can be used only once. Quillstack staff can never see your password and support never asks for it, so always use the self-service reset link.

## Email change confirmation flow

| Step | Action | Timeout |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Enter new email and password in settings | None |
| 2 | Receive confirmation email | Sent immediately |
| 3 | Click confirmation link | Must click within 60 minutes |
| 4 | Email address updated | Takes effect immediately |
