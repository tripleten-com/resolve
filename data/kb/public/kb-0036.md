If a payment fails, Quillstack retries the charge automatically over the next week. If all retries fail, your workspace switches to read-only mode to protect your data while you update your payment method.

## Before you start

Payment failures are usually caused by expired cards, insufficient funds, or bank security holds. You receive an email notification after the first failed attempt and again before the workspace enters read-only mode. Only an Owner or Admin can update the payment method from Settings > Billing > Payment method. Read-only mode blocks all write operations, including creating pages, editing tasks, and uploading files, but you can still view and export your data.

## Steps

**To restore access after a failed payment:**

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com.
2. Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.
3. Click **Update payment method** and enter a valid card.
4. Click **Save and retry payment**.
5. The charge is processed immediately; if successful, full access is restored within 60 seconds.

A workspace in read-only mode after a payment failure keeps all of its data for 30 days, and updating the payment method restores full access immediately. If the payment fails again after you update the card, contact your bank to authorize the charge, then try again.

## How payment retries work

A failed payment is retried automatically 3 times, on day 1, day 3, and day 7 after the failure; if every retry fails the workspace switches to read-only mode. The timeline below shows the retry schedule for a payment that fails on the 1st of the month:

| Day | Event                                      | Workspace status |
|-----|--------------------------------------------|------------------|
| 1   | Payment fails, first retry scheduled       | Full access      |
| 1   | First retry fails                          | Full access      |
| 3   | Second retry fails                         | Full access      |
| 7   | Third retry fails, workspace enters read-only mode | Read-only        |

You receive an email after each failed retry. The final email, sent on day 7, warns that the workspace will enter read-only mode if you do not update your payment method. If you update the payment method before the third retry, the retry is canceled and the new card is charged immediately.

## What happens in read-only mode

In read-only mode, you can view all of your data, download exports, and sign in, but you cannot make any changes. Write operations are blocked across all surfaces:

- Web app: creating or editing pages, tasks, and projects returns an error.
- API: write requests return HTTP 403 with error code QS-1050.
- Mobile app: edits are queued locally but fail to sync and display an error.
- Integrations: webhooks and Slack notifications stop firing; GitHub pull requests no longer link to tasks.

Error QS-1050 means the workspace is in read-only mode, so write requests are rejected. Updating the payment method restores full access and clears all queued operations. Mobile app edits that failed to sync are retried automatically after access is restored.

## Data retention in read-only mode

A workspace in read-only mode keeps all of its data for 30 days. If you do not update the payment method within 30 days, the workspace is scheduled for deletion and you receive a final warning email. After deletion, the workspace is retained for 14 days and can be restored by an Owner from Settings > Workspaces > Deleted. After 14 days it is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Troubleshooting

**I updated my payment method but the workspace is still in read-only mode.** Access is restored within 60 seconds after a successful charge. Sign out and sign back in to refresh your session. If the workspace is still read-only after 5 minutes, the payment may have failed again; check Settings > Billing > Payment method for an error message.

**The payment succeeded but I received another failed payment email.** Email notifications can be delayed by up to 1 hour. If the payment succeeded (confirmed in Settings > Billing > Invoices), ignore the email. If you are unsure, contact support from Settings > Support with your invoice number.

**I am an Admin but cannot update the payment method.** Only Owners can update the payment method on some workspaces, depending on the security settings. Ask the Owner to update the card or to transfer ownership to you temporarily. Ownership transfer is immediate and can be reversed after you update the payment method.

**My API key returns QS-1050 but the workspace is not in read-only mode in the web app.** Sign out of the web app and sign back in to confirm the workspace status. If the web app shows full access but the API still returns QS-1050, the error may be cached; wait 5 minutes and retry the API request. If the error persists, contact support with the request ID from the API response.

**Can I get an extension on the 30-day retention period?** No. The 30-day period is fixed and applies to all workspaces. If you need more time, update your payment method before the 30 days expire, then cancel the subscription if you no longer need the workspace. This keeps your data accessible while you decide what to do.
