Quillstack support can discuss billing details only with the workspace Owner or Admins. This policy protects your account security and ensures that sensitive billing information is shared only with authorized users.

## Before you start

Workspace roles are Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer. Only an Owner can delete the workspace, transfer ownership, or change the billing contact. Admins can manage billing settings, including payment methods and subscriptions, but cannot transfer ownership. Editors and Viewers cannot access billing information.

## Who can discuss billing with support

Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged, and never shares information about another customer's workspace. If you are an Editor or Viewer and need billing information, ask an Owner or Admin to contact support or share the details with you directly.

## What support verifies

When you contact support about billing, the agent verifies your identity by checking that the email address you are writing from matches one of the following:

| Role           | Can discuss billing | Can change payment method | Can cancel subscription |
|----------------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| Owner          | Yes                 | Yes                       | Yes                     |
| Admin          | Yes                 | Yes                       | No                      |
| Editor         | No                  | No                        | No                      |
| Viewer         | No                  | No                        | No                      |

If your email address does not match an Owner or Admin account in the workspace, support cannot share billing details with you. You must ask an Owner or Admin to contact support or grant you Admin access.

## Steps

To verify your role before contacting support:

1. Sign in to app.quillstack.com and open the workspace.
2. Navigate to Settings > Members.
3. Find your name in the member list and check the **Role** column.

If your role is Editor or Viewer and you need billing information, ask an Owner or Admin to contact support or upgrade your role to Admin.

## Troubleshooting

**Support will not discuss my billing issue because I am an Editor.** Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged. Ask an Owner or Admin to contact support on your behalf, or request that they upgrade your role to Admin.

**I am an Admin but support says they cannot verify my identity.** Ensure you are contacting support from the email address associated with your Quillstack account. If you are using a different email address, sign in to app.quillstack.com, navigate to Settings > Account, and verify the email address on file.

**I need billing information for a workspace I do not belong to.** Support never shares information about another customer's workspace. If you need billing details for a workspace where you are not a member, ask the workspace Owner to add you as an Admin or share the information directly.

**Can I authorize someone outside my workspace to discuss billing?** No. Support discusses billing only with Owners and Admins who are members of the workspace. If you need an external accountant or consultant to manage billing, add them to the workspace as an Admin.
