You can upgrade or downgrade your Quillstack plan at any time to access more features or adjust your costs. The timing of the change depends on whether you are moving to a higher or lower plan tier.

## Before you start

Quillstack offers three plans: Free, Team, and Business. Only an Owner or Admin can change the plan from Settings > Billing > Plan. Review the feature differences in the plans comparison article before you make a change. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

## Steps

**To upgrade your plan:**

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Plan.
2. Click **Upgrade** next to the plan you want (Team or Business).
3. Review the prorated charge for the current billing cycle.
4. Enter your payment method if you are upgrading from Free.
5. Click **Confirm upgrade**.

Plan upgrades take effect immediately. You gain access to the new plan's features right away, and your workspace members see the changes within seconds. The prorated charge for the remainder of the current billing cycle appears on your payment method immediately. If you are upgrading from a paid plan to a higher paid plan, any unused portion of your current subscription becomes a credit and reduces the upgrade charge.

**To downgrade your plan:**

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Plan.
2. Click **Downgrade** next to the plan you want (Team or Free).
3. Review the features you will lose and the effective date.
4. Click **Confirm downgrade**.

Plan downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You keep full access to your current plan until the cycle ends, and no refund is issued for the remaining time. If downgrading would put you over the new plan's limits (for example, more than 3 members on Free), you must remove members or workspaces before the downgrade is processed.

## Plan limits and feature changes

The table below shows the most common limits and features that change when you upgrade or downgrade:

| Feature                     | Free      | Team         | Business      |
|-----------------------------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Members                     | 3         | Unlimited    | Unlimited     |
| Workspaces                  | 1         | Unlimited    | Unlimited     |
| File storage per workspace  | 500 MB    | 50 GB        | 500 GB        |
| Public page links           | No        | Yes          | Yes           |
| API key expiry              | 30 days   | 90 days      | 365 days      |
| SSO (SAML/OIDC)             | No        | No           | Yes           |
| Audit log                   | No        | No           | Yes           |

When you downgrade, any data that exceeds the new plan's limits becomes read-only. For example, if you downgrade from Team to Free with 50 GB of files, you can still view all files but cannot upload new ones until you delete enough to get under 500 MB. Pages with public links stop being accessible to non-members when you downgrade from Team or Business to Free.

## Billing and proration

Upgrades charge you immediately for the prorated cost of the new plan for the remainder of your billing cycle. If you upgrade from Team ($12 per member per month) to Business ($28 per member per month) with 15 days left in a 30-day cycle, you are charged the difference ($16 per member) prorated for 15 days, which is $8 per member. Your next invoice bills the full Business rate.

Downgrades do not generate refunds or credits. You continue to pay for your current plan until the end of the billing cycle, then the new plan's rate applies starting with the next invoice. If you downgrade from annual to monthly billing, the change takes effect at the end of your annual term and the next invoice uses monthly pricing.

## Troubleshooting

**The upgrade button is grayed out.** Your payment method may have failed or expired. Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and update your card, then try the upgrade again. If your workspace is in read-only mode, updating the payment method restores access and allows the upgrade.

**I downgraded but still see features from my old plan.** Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Check Settings > Billing > Plan for the effective date. You keep access to all features until that date.

**I downgraded and now I cannot access my workspace.** If your downgrade put you over the new plan's member limit, you must remove members to regain access. An Owner can sign in and remove members from Settings > Members, even if the workspace is otherwise restricted.

**I upgraded by mistake and want a refund.** Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge and require review by the billing team. Contact support from Settings > Support with your invoice number and reason for the request. The billing team replies within 3 business days.

**Can I switch from monthly to annual billing mid-cycle?** Yes. Changing billing frequency is treated as an upgrade: the unused portion of your monthly subscription becomes a credit, and you are charged the prorated annual rate for the remainder of the year. The change takes effect immediately.
