This register lists known issues across Quillstack, including workarounds, affected versions, and escalation paths. Use this page to triage support tickets and determine whether a customer report matches an existing bug. Update this register when a new issue is confirmed by engineering or when an issue is resolved.

## How to use this register

Check this register before escalating a ticket. If the customer's report matches a known issue, apply the documented workaround and link the ticket to the tracking issue in the bug tracker. If the issue is not listed here and you suspect a product defect, escalate to #support-escalations with the ticket number and reproduction steps. Engineering will create a tracking issue and add it to this register within 1 business day.

Link customers to the public article [kb-0075](kb-0075) when appropriate, but never share issue IDs or internal timelines.

## Escalation criteria

Escalate to #support-escalations immediately if:
- The customer reports data loss (missing pages, tasks, or attachments)
- The issue affects billing or prevents payment method updates
- The customer is on Business plan and the issue blocks their SSO sign-in
- The issue affects > 10 customers in a 24-hour period

For all other issues, escalate if the workaround does not resolve the customer's problem or if the issue is not listed in this register.

## Updating this register

When engineering confirms a new issue, add it to the Active table with:
- Issue ID from the bug tracker (QS-NNNN)
- Symptom: what the customer sees or reports
- Affected versions: be specific (e.g., "API v2.1.0–2.1.2" not "API v2")
- Workaround: what to tell the customer, or "None" if no workaround exists
- ETA: target fix date from engineering, or "TBD" if not yet scheduled

When an issue is resolved, move it to the Recently Resolved table and notify #customer-support. Update [kb-0075](kb-0075) if the issue was listed there.
