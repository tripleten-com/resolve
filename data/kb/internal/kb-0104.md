This document defines when and how to escalate support tickets, who to involve for different issue types, and what response times to maintain. Use this matrix whenever a ticket cannot be resolved at tier 1 or requires approval, specialist review, or out-of-hours action.

## Before you start

Confirm the workspace plan in the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal before escalating. Response targets and escalation paths differ by plan: Free receives replies within 3 business days, Team within 1 business day, and Business within 4 business hours. Check the ticket priority tag in the helpdesk; P0 and P1 tickets bypass normal queues and go directly to the on-call engineer.

## Escalation paths by issue type

The table below lists common issue categories, the team or individual to escalate to, the channel to use, and any special handling notes.

| Issue type | Escalate to | Channel | Notes |
|------------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Billing dispute or chargeback | Billing team | #billing-escalations | Tag @billing-lead; include invoice number and workspace ID |
| Refund request | Billing team | Assign to Billing queue in helpdesk | Billing team reviews and replies within 3 business days; never issue refunds directly |
| Failed payment, read-only mode | Billing team | #billing-escalations | Urgent if Business plan; check payment processor logs first |
| Security vulnerability report | Security team | security@quillstack.com | Forward the original report; acknowledge within 2 business days; do not run a bug bounty |
| Data breach or compromise | Security team, on-call lead | Page on-call + #security-incidents | Follow playbook kb-0107; notify legal within 1 hour |
| API outage or QS-1099 spike | On-call engineer | Page on-call + #incidents | Follow runbook kb-0101; post status updates every 15 minutes |
| GDPR deletion request | Privacy team | #privacy-requests | Follow procedure kb-0110; confirm identity before proceeding |
| SSO setup or SAML issue | Enterprise support | #enterprise-support | Business plan only; pair with customer on Zoom if needed |
| Export timeout or QS-1077 | Engineering | #export-jobs | Check job ID in admin console; retry manually if stuck |
| Bulk import failure | Engineering | #import-jobs | Attach import logs; most failures are resolved by re-uploading archive |
| Pricing override or discount | Sales operations | #sales-ops | Requires VP approval for discounts over 20%; document in CRM |
| Account locked, cannot reset password | Tier 2 support | Assign to Tier 2 queue | Verify identity via original signup email before unlocking |
| Webhook delivery issues (QS-1061) | Engineering | #webhooks | Check delivery logs; most are customer endpoint issues, not our bug |

## Response and resolution targets

All tickets must receive a first reply within the SLA for the workspace plan. Business tickets have a 4-hour first-response target; if you cannot resolve the issue in that window, send an acknowledgment with next steps and an ETA. Team tickets must receive a first reply within 1 business day, and Free tickets within 3 business days.

For P0 incidents (API down, data loss, security breach), page the on-call engineer immediately using PagerDuty. Post an initial status update in #incidents within 15 minutes and continue updates every 15 minutes until resolved. P1 tickets (single workspace broken, payment processor down) require a reply within 1 hour on Business plans and 4 hours on Team or Free.

## When to page on-call

Page the on-call engineer for P0 incidents only: complete API outage, database unavailable, authentication broken for all users, or confirmed security breach. Do not page for individual workspace issues, slow exports, or single-customer bugs unless the customer is on a Business annual plan and the issue blocks their entire team.

The on-call rotation is posted in #support-oncall and updates every Monday. If the on-call engineer does not respond within 10 minutes, escalate to the engineering manager listed in the PagerDuty schedule.

## Approval requirements

Refunds require billing team approval and are never issued by support staff. The billing team reviews every refund request and replies within 3 business days. Pricing discounts over 20% require VP approval; submit the request in #sales-ops with the CRM opportunity link and business justification.

Manual data deletions (outside the normal GDPR process) require legal and security approval. Open a ticket in #privacy-requests and wait for written confirmation before proceeding. Account unlocks after suspicious activity require tier 2 review; never unlock an account based solely on the customer's request without verifying identity through the original signup email or a recent invoice.

## Handoff notes

When escalating a ticket, include the workspace ID, plan, ticket number, and a one-sentence summary of the issue and what you have already tried. Tag the relevant team lead in the Slack channel and assign the ticket to the correct queue in the helpdesk. Do not leave the ticket in your personal queue after escalating.

For security reports sent to security@quillstack.com, acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and forward to #security-incidents. Quillstack does not run a paid bug bounty program; thank the reporter and provide an ETA for triage, but do not promise compensation.
