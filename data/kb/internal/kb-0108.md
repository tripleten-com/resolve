A billing dispute or chargeback is when a customer asks their bank to reverse a charge instead of contacting Quillstack support. This guide explains how to identify disputes, what to check before responding, and how to escalate chargebacks to the billing and legal teams.

## Before you start

You need access to the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal and the billing dashboard. Disputes appear in the #billing-alerts Slack channel and in the helpdesk under the Billing Dispute queue. Only the billing team can respond to a chargeback; support engineers gather context and document the case.

Check the customer's plan and workspace status in the admin console. If the workspace is deleted or the charge is older than 90 days, tag the case as Historical and assign it to the billing team without further investigation.

## Steps

1. Open the dispute ticket in the helpdesk and locate the invoice number (format INV-YYYYMM-NNNNN) and charge date.
2. In the admin console, search for the workspace by billing email or invoice number.
3. Verify that the person who opened the dispute is the Owner or an Admin of the workspace. Support discusses billing details only with the Owner or Admins of the workspace that was charged, and never shares information about another customer's workspace. If the requester is not authorized, reply with macro BILLING_AUTH_REQUIRED and close the ticket.
4. Check the charge history for the workspace. If the same card was charged the same amount twice within 24 hours, the second charge is a duplicate; after review, the billing team reverses it within 5 business days. Tag the case as Duplicate Charge and assign it to the billing team immediately.
5. If the charge is not a duplicate, check whether the dispute was filed within 14 days of the charge. Refunds are only possible within 14 days of a charge. If the dispute is outside this window, note "Outside refund window" in the internal case log.
6. Document the workspace activity in the case: number of active members, last sign-in date, number of pages and tasks created in the billing cycle, and whether the workspace is still active or has been deleted.
7. Check for prior disputes or refunds on the same workspace. If this is a repeat dispute, note the earlier case numbers and escalate to the billing team with priority High.
8. Assign the case to the billing team queue and add the tag Chargeback if the bank initiated the dispute, or Refund Request if the customer contacted Quillstack first.

## Chargeback evidence requirements

When a bank initiates a chargeback, the billing team has 7 days to submit evidence. The evidence packet must include the invoice, proof of service (sign-in logs and workspace activity), the terms of service the customer accepted, and any prior correspondence about the charge. The billing team assembles this packet, but support provides the activity logs from the admin console.

Use the admin console to export the workspace activity report: go to Workspaces > [workspace name] > Activity > Export (last 90 days). Save the CSV as workspace-activity-[workspace-id].csv and attach it to the case. If the workspace is deleted, pull the activity from the archive database using the script at scripts/export-deleted-workspace-activity.sh and attach the output.

## Fraud and stolen card disputes

If the dispute reason is "unauthorized transaction" or "card stolen," escalate immediately to the fraud team by paging #security-incidents. Do not reply to the customer or close the case. The fraud team reviews the sign-in IP addresses, device fingerprints, and 2FA status to determine whether the account was compromised. If fraud is confirmed, the billing team does not contest the chargeback.

Mark the case with the tag Fraud Investigation and add a note with the workspace ID, billing email, and dispute reason. The fraud team replies within 4 hours during business hours or 12 hours on weekends.

## Escalation and resolution

Most disputes are resolved within 10 business days. The billing team updates the case in the helpdesk when the bank makes a decision. If Quillstack wins the dispute, the charge stands and the customer is notified by email. If the bank sides with the customer, the charge is reversed and the workspace is downgraded to Free or suspended if the account balance is negative.

If a customer opens a support ticket after losing a dispute, use macro DISPUTE_FINAL and direct them to billing@quillstack.com for further review. Do not reopen the dispute or offer a refund; all post-dispute requests go to the billing director.

The following table shows common dispute reasons and how to categorize them:

| Dispute reason               | Helpdesk tag       | Action                          |
|------------------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------|
| Duplicate charge             | Duplicate Charge   | Assign to billing team (High)   |
| Unrecognized charge          | Chargeback         | Gather evidence, assign billing |
| Service not received         | Chargeback         | Export activity, assign billing |
| Canceled subscription        | Refund Request     | Check cancellation date, assign |
| Unauthorized transaction     | Fraud Investigation| Page #security-incidents        |
| Outside 14-day refund window | Refund Denied      | Note in log, assign billing     |
