This procedure covers how to handle data deletion requests under GDPR Article 17 (right to erasure) and similar privacy regulations. A valid request requires identity verification and may have exemptions. Follow every step to ensure compliance and avoid re-identification risk.

## Before you start

Data deletion requests arrive through the privacy@quillstack.com inbox or are escalated from support tickets. Only the privacy team (legal@quillstack.com) and on-call engineers with admin console access can process these requests. You must verify the requester's identity before taking any action. If the request mentions an active paid subscription, page #legal-privacy in Slack before proceeding—billing obligations may delay or limit deletion.

Quillstack retains deleted content for recovery periods: deleted pages stay in Trash for 30 days, and a deleted workspace is retained for 14 days and can be restored by an Owner from Settings > Workspaces > Deleted. After these windows close, content is permanently deleted from production and backups. The audit log is Business-only, retained for 365 days, and exportable as CSV from Settings > Security > Audit log; it may contain the requester's email and actions even after workspace deletion.

## Steps

1. **Verify identity.** Reply to the requester from privacy@quillstack.com and ask them to confirm the email address on their Quillstack account and the workspace name or URL. If they cannot provide both, request a government-issued ID or send a one-time verification link from the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal. Log the verification method in the ticket.
2. **Check for exemptions.** Open the admin console and search for the account by email. Review the Notes field for flags such as `legal_hold`, `active_dispute`, or `fraud_investigation`. If any flag is present, forward the request to legal@quillstack.com and do not proceed. Check the workspace billing status: if the subscription is active and not canceled, reply that deletion can proceed only after cancellation completes at the end of the current cycle, and provide the cycle end date from the Billing tab.
3. **Locate all data.** In the admin console, go to Accounts > Search and enter the requester's email. Note every workspace where they appear as a member, including workspaces they own and workspaces where they are a Viewer. Export the list as CSV and attach it to ticket QS-GDPR-NNNN in the bug tracker. Check the API Keys tab: if the account has issued any API keys, note the workspace IDs and key prefixes.
4. **Remove the account from shared workspaces.** For each workspace where the requester is not the Owner, navigate to the workspace in the admin console and click Members > Remove. This removes their access immediately. The workspace Owner is notified by email. Do not delete pages or projects created by the requester in shared workspaces—those remain owned by the workspace.
5. **Delete owned workspaces.** For each workspace where the requester is the Owner, click Workspaces > Delete in the admin console. Confirm the deletion. The workspace enters the 14-day retention window and will be purged automatically after that period. If the requester asks for immediate permanent deletion, use the Force Delete option, which bypasses the retention window and removes all data within 24 hours. Log `force_delete: true` in the ticket.
6. **Purge audit logs and backups.** After workspace deletion, the requester's email and actions may still appear in other workspaces' audit logs (if those workspaces are on the Business plan). Use the admin console Audit Purge tool: enter the requester's email and select Purge from all workspaces. This runs a background job that removes matching log entries within 48 hours. For backup purges, file issue QS-OPS-PURGE in the bug tracker with the account email and deletion timestamp; the infrastructure team will scrub the email from incremental and full backups within 7 days.
7. **Revoke API keys and sessions.** In the admin console, go to API Keys and click Revoke all for the requester's account. Then navigate to Sessions > Active and click Sign out everywhere. Both actions take effect immediately.
8. **Confirm completion.** After all workspaces are deleted, audit logs purged, and the backup purge ticket closed, reply to the requester from privacy@quillstack.com. Use this template: "We have completed your data deletion request. Your account, workspaces, and personal data have been removed from Quillstack systems. Residual references in backups will be purged within 7 days. If you have questions, contact privacy@quillstack.com." Close the ticket and mark it `gdpr_completed` in the bug tracker.

## Troubleshooting

If the requester is the Owner of a workspace with other members, deletion will fail with error `workspace_has_members`. Reply that they must first transfer ownership (kb-0017) or remove all other members before we can delete the workspace. If the workspace is on an active annual Business plan, check with #legal-privacy whether the contract allows early deletion or whether the requester must wait until the contract term ends. For requests where the email does not match any account, search by workspace name and check the Members list manually; reply "No account found" only after confirming the email is absent from every workspace.
