This policy governs when the billing team approves a refund request and what to tell the customer. Every refund request is reviewed by the billing team within 3 business days, and refunds are never issued automatically or by the support assistant. Follow the decision tree below and use the standard reply templates from the helpdesk.

## Before you start

Check the charge date in the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal under Billing > Transactions. Refunds are only possible within 14 days of the charge. If the request arrives after that window, the answer is no: decline politely and offer a credit or proration instead if the customer is downgrading or canceling.

Verify the requester is an Owner or Admin of the workspace that was charged. If they are not, do not discuss the charge and direct them to kb-0042.

## Decision tree

Use this table to decide whether to approve:

| Scenario | Refund? | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Duplicate charge (same card, same amount, within 24 hours) | Yes | Reverse the duplicate within 5 business days; log as duplicate in admin console |
| Request within 14 days, first refund for this workspace | Yes | Full refund; note in workspace record |
| Request within 14 days, second or later refund for same workspace | Escalate | Forward to #billing-escalations for approval |
| Request after 14 days | No | Offer credit or proration; cite refund window |
| Customer removed seats mid-cycle | No | Explain credit will apply to next invoice; removed seats are never refunded in cash |
| Failed payment triggered read-only mode, now resolved | No | No refund; access was restored when payment succeeded |
| Customer claims they did not authorize the charge | Escalate | Forward to #billing-disputes and cc legal@ |

## Duplicate charges

If the same card was charged the same amount twice within 24 hours, treat it as a duplicate. Check the transaction log in the admin console: if both charges have status `succeeded` and timestamps less than 24 hours apart, reverse the second charge within 5 business days. Tag the transaction with `duplicate_reversed` and reply with macro REFUND_DUPLICATE_APPROVED. No further approval is needed.

## Seat removal and credits

Removing a seat mid-cycle creates a credit that is applied to the next invoice. Customers often expect a cash refund, but the answer is always no. Use macro REFUND_SEAT_REMOVAL_CREDIT and show them the credit amount under Settings > Billing > Credits in their workspace. If they insist, escalate to #billing-escalations with context.

## Escalations

Escalate to #billing-escalations if the customer has already received one refund in the past 12 months, if they are disputing the charge as unauthorized, or if the situation does not match any row in the decision tree. Include the workspace ID, charge ID, request date, and your recommendation. The billing lead replies within 1 business day.

## What to say

Use these macros from the helpdesk:

- REFUND_APPROVED: standard approval, includes refund timeline (5 to 10 business days)
- REFUND_DECLINED_WINDOW: request is outside the 14-day window
- REFUND_DUPLICATE_APPROVED: duplicate charge reversal
- REFUND_SEAT_REMOVAL_CREDIT: explain credit for removed seats
- REFUND_ESCALATED: tell the customer the billing team is reviewing and will reply within 3 business days

Never promise a specific refund date. Always say "within 5 to 10 business days" and remind them the refund appears on their card statement, not as a Quillstack credit.
