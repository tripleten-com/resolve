Quillstack support and the billing team can apply pricing overrides and discounts to workspace subscriptions when approved by the finance lead. This article documents the types of overrides available, the approval workflow, how to apply them in the admin console, and how to communicate them to customers.

## Before you start

- Only members of the billing team and support leads have access to the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal.
- Every pricing override requires approval from the finance lead before it is applied.
- Discounts are recorded in the customer notes field and must include the approval ticket (for example FIN-8821) and the expiry date if applicable.
- Standard pricing is $12 per member per month for Team monthly, $28 per member per month for Business monthly, and 10 times the monthly rate for annual plans (equivalent to two months free).

## Override types

The admin console supports three categories of pricing override:

| Override type | Applies to | Maximum discount | Typical use case |
|---------------|------------|------------------|------------------|
| Volume discount | Annual subscriptions with 50+ seats | 25% off list price | Large enterprise deals |
| Non-profit discount | Any plan, any billing cycle | 40% off list price | Verified 501(c)(3) organizations |
| Retention credit | Monthly or annual subscriptions | $500 per year | Customer retention after escalation |

Volume discounts are tiered: 10% for 50-99 seats, 15% for 100-199 seats, 20% for 200-499 seats, and 25% for 500+ seats. These tiers are guidelines only; the finance lead may approve a different percentage based on the deal size and contract length.

Non-profit discounts require proof of tax-exempt status uploaded to the workspace record in the admin console. The discount applies for the lifetime of the subscription as long as the organization remains verified.

Retention credits are one-time adjustments applied as a credit to the next invoice. They do not change the per-seat price and expire after 12 months if not used.

## Steps

### Apply a pricing override

1. Obtain approval from the finance lead by creating a ticket in the billing queue with the workspace ID, current plan, requested override type, discount percentage or credit amount, and business justification.
2. Wait for the finance lead to approve the ticket and add the FIN- reference number to the ticket comments.
3. Sign in to the admin console at admin.quillstack.internal and search for the workspace by ID or billing email.
4. Navigate to the Billing tab and click **Add override**.
5. Select the override type from the dropdown, enter the discount percentage or credit amount, and paste the approval ticket number into the Notes field.
6. If the override has an expiry date (for example a 12-month retention credit), set the **Expires on** field to that date; otherwise leave it blank for a permanent override.
7. Click **Apply override** and verify that the new pricing appears in the Subscription summary section.

### Communicate the override to the customer

After applying the override, reply to the customer using the pricing-override macro in the helpdesk. Customize the macro to include the discount percentage or credit amount, the effective date, and the new per-seat price or total invoice amount.

For non-profit discounts, include this sentence: "This discount will remain active as long as your organization's tax-exempt status is verified in our records."

For retention credits, include this sentence: "This credit will be applied to your next invoice and expires 12 months from today if not used."

Do not mention the approval ticket number or internal pricing policies in customer-facing messages.

## Removing or modifying an override

If an override needs to be removed (for example if a non-profit loses its tax-exempt status) or modified (for example if a customer adds seats and moves into a higher volume discount tier), create a new ticket in the billing queue and request approval from the finance lead before making changes in the admin console.

To remove an override, navigate to the workspace Billing tab, find the override in the Active overrides list, and click **Remove**. The workspace will return to standard pricing on the next billing cycle.

To modify an override, remove the existing override and apply a new one following the steps above. The new override takes effect immediately for monthly subscriptions and at the next renewal for annual subscriptions.

## Escalation and edge cases

If a customer requests a discount that exceeds the maximum for the override type, escalate to the finance lead and the VP of Sales by tagging them in the ticket. Do not promise the customer a specific discount until it is approved.

If a customer requests a custom contract with payment terms other than credit card or bank transfer (for example purchase order with net-60 terms), escalate to the sales team using #sales-ops. Support cannot approve custom payment terms.
